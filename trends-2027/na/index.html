Outdoor Living Trends 2027

Every Outdoor Room Starts As A Sketch

The ones people use all year have heat drawn in from the first line.

Outdoor living is moving from the yard into the floor plan, and from summer into every season. Architects rank the outdoor room first, falls are warmer, cities are keeping patios open through winter and homes are going electric.

The next step is comfort. An outdoor room is only as useful as the hours it stays warm – and that is where heat becomes essential.
