# A03 — Sizing calculator ("How many heaters does your space need?")

**Asset:** A03 · **Cell:** RES-CO / ARC-CO · **Status:** draft 0.1 · **Reviewers:** Brian Kim (logic, form), 454 Creative (embed), Mike Clifford (every product value)

## What it does
The visitor enters length, width, enclosure (open / partially covered / covered), climate (mild / cool / cold), fuel and style
preference, mount height and number of zones. On screen they see a teaser: the heater count, the lead model and its controls bundle.
The full report (every model that fits, the controls for each, electrical load, the assumptions) sits behind the lead form.

## How it counts
`count = ceil(area ÷ (nominal coverage × enclosure × climate × mount height × layout))`. Built-in heaters take the mount-height
factor; freestanding heaters do not. A space with less than 30% of its perimeter permanently open is not treated as outdoor and is
routed to the design team. Coming-soon models (Tungsten Coastal, Select) show a range, never a single count. Models without confirmed
coverage show "the Bromic design team sizes this model". Every result carries its controls bundle: Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off,
Dimmer or Eclipse controller, wall switch and app. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity controls, sold separately.

## Files
| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `config.json` | Every number the tool uses. **Edit this**, then `node tools/build-config.mjs` |
| `logic.js` | Pure functions (`defaults`, `normalise`, `size`, `controlsBundle`, …). No DOM, no imports |
| `index.html` | The page. Brand CSS, config, logic and the lead gate are inlined between `@inline` markers by `tools/build-config.mjs`, so it runs from file:// or an iframe with no network |
| `config.js` | Generated from `config.json` (do not edit) |
| `test.mjs` | Unit tests; `node app/test/tools.mjs` runs them |

Embed: `index.html?embed=1` drops the outer margins for an iframe. UTM parameters on the URL are carried into the lead payload.
On submit the page shows the full report and logs the payload to the console. **The HubSpot form embed replaces that** (see
`forms/hubspot-form-spec.md`).
