# A20 — Five-year cost of patio heating (TCO model)

Draft 0.1.0. Cell COM-CO, gated (lead form from `../_shared/lead.js`), CTA → A21 ROI calculator.
Compares five years of owning and running electric, natural gas and propane patio heating for an operator.

The visitor picks a venue picture (a starting profile of seats and season, every part a placeholder they change), the size, a
state (EIA prices fill in, editable) and the months, days a week and hours a day heated. The result card shows totals, fuel a
day, a running-total chart with the payback year (`crossovers`), a breakdown bar and propane cylinder swaps. Costs, dimming and
cylinder details sit in a closed Fine-tune panel.

## The maths

```
Fuel per hour   electric     kW × heaters × output% × $/kWh
                natural gas  BTU/hr × heaters × output% ÷ 100,000 × $/therm
                propane      BTU/hr × heaters × output% ÷ 91,500 × $/gal
Heaters         blank → ceil(area ÷ (coverage × cover factor × climate factor)); freestanding gas never above
                4 seats a heater: max(that, ceil(seats ÷ 4)); area = seats × 20 ft², or ft² entered
Fuel, 5 years   Σ (fuel per hour × hours a year) × (1 + escalation)^year, years 0–4
Propane swaps   round(gallons a year ÷ usable gallons per cylinder)
Handling        swaps × minutes ÷ 60 × wage × 5   (not escalated)
Total           heaters × (purchase + install) + heaters × maintenance × 5 + fuel + handling
```

Out of scope (stated on the page): demand charges, utility connection fees, financing, tax, resale.

## Test case 1 — COM-CO-01's five-year example

Inputs from the article (templates/calculation-model.md, not the config defaults): 40 seats (800 ft²), partly covered, cool
(factor 0.85); heater counts sized by the model, 7 × Platinum 4500W (800 ÷ (143 × 0.85) = 6.6) and 10 × Tungsten
Freestanding Gas on natural gas and on propane (40 seats ÷ 4); 1,000 h a year; 14.07¢/kWh, $1.01/therm, $2.46/gal; 4.7 gal
cylinders, 10 min a swap, $25/h; purchase, install and maintenance set to 0 (the article leaves them as [[VERIFY]]).

| | Electric | Natural gas | Propane |

| Energy a year | 31,500 kWh | 3,800 therms | 4,153 gal |
| Fuel a year | $4,432 | $3,838 | $10,216 |

Test 2 checks the sizing rule: 858 ft² at the catalogue coverage gives 6 electric heaters by area and 11 freestanding gas
heaters by seats (858 ÷ 20 = 42.9 seats ÷ 4).

## Placeholders

Purchase, install and maintenance per heater are **placeholders, not Bromic prices** — the page tags them "Placeholder" at the
inputs, in the teaser and in the report. Heater hours, ft² per seat and minutes per swap are also placeholders.

## Rates differ from the article

The config defaults are newer EIA figures than COM-CO-01 uses: electricity 13.54¢ (May 2026) vs the article's 14.07¢
(Jan–May 2026); natural gas $0.87/therm (Jan–May 2026) vs $1.01 (Jan–Apr 2025); propane $2.674 (week of 30 Mar 2026) vs $2.46
(winter 2025–26 forecast). Finance should pick one set for the article, A20 and A21 (A21 uses 14.07¢).

## Controls

Electric: Affinity Smart-Heat Dimmer or On/Off controller and a wall switch per zone; Bromic Smart-Heat app. Gas options carry
`[[VERIFY: control options for Tungsten Freestanding Gas — Product team]]`. Wi-Fi and app features require compatible Affinity
Smart-Heat controls, sold separately — shown in the teaser and the report.
