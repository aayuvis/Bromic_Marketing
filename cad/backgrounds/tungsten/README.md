# Tungsten background bank

Real photographs of Tungsten installations (or with no heater), the only backgrounds allowed for Tungsten Series II CAD composites
(see [../../CAD-TO-REAL-REQUIREMENTS.md](../../CAD-TO-REAL-REQUIREMENTS.md)). These are the largest originals held, copied unaltered from
bromic.com. Rights stay `[[VERIFY: image rights — Marketing]]`, as in the image library.

## What the quality check found

- **38 photos kept**, out of 45 candidates.
  - 7 were dropped: wrong product, CGI render or yacht.
  - Duplicates were removed with a perceptual-hash check.
- **None is 4K.**
  - The largest Tungsten originals published by Bromic are 2,560 px on the long side.
  - 4K+ backgrounds need new photography or the original camera files; the agency or photographer will have them.
  - Nothing here has been upscaled, because upscaling invents detail.
- Each photo was checked three ways:
  1. automatically, for resolution, sharpness (Laplacian variance) and clipped highlights or shadows;
  2. on a contact sheet, for product and scene;
  3. at full size, for each flagged photo.
