## 01 · The Alfresco Goes All-Electric
**Electrify · Pre-Wire · Comply**
Victoria requires new homes and most new commercial buildings to be all-electric from 1 January 2027. The ACT has blocked new gas network connections since December 2023. City of Sydney's all-electric rules for new homes began in January 2026 and from 2027 extend to outdoor gas appliances and large hotels.
- Victoria: no gas connection in new-dwelling permits since 1 Jan 2024; all-electric new builds from 1 Jan 2027
- ACT: new gas network connections blocked since 8 Dec 2023
- City of Sydney: outdoor gas appliances in new homes and hotels over 100 rooms from 1 Jan 2027; six other NSW councils ban gas in new homes

**Hero stat:** 1 Jan 2027 – the date a new Victorian alfresco has no gas line to run a heater from
**The Implication:** *Architects:* specify electric radiant heat at DA stage. *Builders:* run heater circuits in the alfresco ceiling at frame stage. *Homeowners:* in a new Victorian home, outdoor heat will be electric. *Hospitality:* new venues plan electric heat from the first drawing.
**Bromic Insight:** Electric infrared is the outdoor heat built for an all-electric Australia.

## 02 · Solar-Powered Evenings
**Generate · Store · Warm**
About 40% of Australian homes have rooftop solar, and home batteries are surging. With electricity prices up as rebates ended, households want heat that is efficient and controllable.
- 28.3 GW of rooftop solar across about 4.3m installations (end 2025); a record 1 GW installed in Q2 2026
- 454,753 home batteries installed by end 2025; 183,245 in the second half alone
- Electricity prices +13.2% in the year to August 2026 (ABS CPI)

**Hero stat:** ~40% of homes generate their own power (Australian Energy Council, 2025)
**The Implication:** *Homeowners:* stored solar can run evening heat; dimming and zoning keep the draw low. *Builders:* put outdoor heating on the home-energy plan. *Hospitality:* time heat to service hours.
**Bromic Insight:** Dimmable, zoned infrared heats people, not the air, so every kilowatt counts.
*Visual: day-to-night strip – panels by day, battery, heater glowing at night.*

## 03 · The Renovation-Led Alfresco
**Upgrade · Retrofit · Extend**
Australians are improving the homes they have. Renovation spend is near its peak, renovation lending is almost three times pre-pandemic, and the existing patio is the next room to finish.
- $56.2bn renovation spend in 2025-26, 37% of housing spend (KPMG)
- Renovation lending almost 3× pre-pandemic; activity growing twice as fast as the economy (HIA)
- NSW renovation investment expected to outpace Victoria's by nearly 50% in 2026 (HIA)

**Hero stat:** $56.2bn – renovation spend, near the 2021-22 peak
**The Implication:** *Builders:* the biggest market is the patio that already exists. *Architects:* detail surface-mount and retrofit options. *Homeowners:* add a roof, light and heat to the alfresco you have.
**Bromic Insight:** A heater mounts to the patio you have, turning it into a room you use all year.

## 04 · Smaller Lots, Harder-Working Outdoor Space
**Compact · Elevate · Zone**
Lots are shrinking while houses hold their size, and apartments are rising. Buyers search for outdoor areas and balconies anyway.
- Median land 645 m² in 2025, from 670 m² in 2015; average new house 241 m² (PropTrack)
- Apartment approvals +13.2% in 2025-26 (ABS); multi-unit starts forecast near 97,000 by 2028 (HIA)
- "Balcony" was the 7th most-searched keyword; "courtyard" searches +62% (PropTrack)

**Hero stat:** +62% – growth in "courtyard" searches in 2025 (PropTrack)
**The Implication:** *Designers:* wall, ceiling and recessed heat keeps small courtyards and balconies free. *Developers:* heated balconies and rooftops as a selling point; strata rules favour electric. *Homeowners:* one well-placed heater makes a courtyard a nightly room.
**Bromic Insight:** Low-clearance and recessed heat fits the spaces Australians are building now.

## 05 · The Roof Over The Alfresco
**Shelter · Mount · Integrate**
Pergolas and patio roofs dominate the outdoor structures market, and pavilions are the fastest-growing segment. Every roof is a mounting surface for heat.
- Pergolas and patios: 70.4% of Australian outdoor structure revenue in 2025
- Market US$54.2M in 2026, to US$86.5M by 2033 (6.9% a year)
- Australia grows faster than the world (6.1%) and is more pergola-led (70% vs 64%)

**Hero stat:** 6.9% a year – Australia's outdoor structures market outpaces the world (Grand View Research)
**The Implication:** *Patio builders and pergola makers:* bundle heat, light and power with the roof. *Architects:* detail heaters into louvre and pergola framing. *Homeowners:* plan the heat with the roof, not after.
**Bromic Insight:** Every pergola and louvred roof is a place for heat.
*Visual: exploded pergola – roof, light, heat, controls.*

## 06 · Milder Winters, Still-Cold Nights
**Evening · Winter · Comfort**
Winter 2026 was the warmest on record in Sydney, Melbourne and Canberra. Days are milder and drier, which means more evenings worth being outside, yet nights still fall cold.
- Warmest winter on record in Sydney (15.29 °C), Melbourne (12.18 °C) and Canberra (8.14 °C) (Weatherzone, provisional)
- On 6 July 2026 Melbourne fell to 3.2 °C and five states dropped below zero
- Cool-season rainfall down about 16% in the southwest since 1970 and 9% in the southeast since 1994 (CSIRO/BOM)

**Hero stat:** 3.2 °C – Melbourne's coldest night of 2026, in its warmest winter on record
**The Implication:** Drier, milder winters bring more usable evenings; radiant heat closes the gap after sunset. *Hospitality:* winter covers on the terrace. *Homeowners:* the alfresco works in July.
**Bromic Insight:** The Australian winter is made for outdoor living, once the evening chill is handled.
*Visual: winter evening temperature curve, with a warm zone under a heater.*

## 07 · Alfresco Is Policy
**Permit · Extend · Stay**
Cities are making outdoor dining permanent. City of Sydney has waived outdoor dining fees for good, NSW has relaxed the rules, and Melbourne's parklets are permanent.
- City of Sydney: nearly 1,000 approvals, 800+ businesses, about 13,000 m² of outdoor area; fees waived permanently
- More than a third of those businesses saw turnover rise 20%
- NSW: 580+ businesses use relaxed outdoor dining rules; about 150 Sydney on-street areas to become permanent footpath extensions

**Hero stat:** 1 in 3 Sydney outdoor-dining businesses grew turnover by 20% (City of Sydney, 2025)
**The Implication:** Permanent outdoor seats need permanent, permitted comfort. *Operators:* treat heated seats as fixed capacity. *Designers:* specify durable fixed heat, not portables.
**Bromic Insight:** Fixed electric heat turns a permanent permit into a year-round seat.

## 08 · Every Outdoor Seat Must Earn Its Keep
**Covers · Margin · Control**
Hospitality turns over $66bn, but closures and defaults are high. A heated outdoor seat used all year is one of the few ways to add capacity without adding rent.
- Cafés, restaurants and takeaway turnover $66.3bn in FY2024-25, +2.5% (ABS via R&CA)
- 10.4% of foodservice businesses closed in a year, the highest of any sector (R&CA)
- Café and restaurant payment default rate 1.15% vs 0.31% nationally (CreditorWatch)

**Hero stat:** $66bn – the hospitality spend outdoor seats compete for
**The Implication:** *Operators:* heating must pay back in seat-hours; zoned control keeps running costs down. *Designers:* zone heat by table and bar.
**Bromic Insight:** A heated seat earns through winter; zoned control means you pay only for the seats in use.

## 09 · Connected Comfort
**Control · Scene · Schedule**
Smart homes are now the Australian norm, and households budget for them.
- 86% of households own a smart home product (June 2026), up from 69% in 2022
- 71% have a smart-home budget, averaging more than $3,500
- Market to pass $11bn in 2026 (Telsyte)

**Hero stat:** 86% – Australian homes with smart tech (Telsyte, 2026)
**The Implication:** *Integrators:* add heat zones to the outdoor scene. *Homeowners:* dim, zone and schedule from an app.
**Bromic Insight:** Affinity Smart-Heat controls dim, zone and schedule heat with the rest of the home. App and Wi-Fi features need compatible Affinity controls, sold separately.

## 10 · Coastal, Seamless, Indoor-Outdoor
**Coast · Open · Disappear**
Australians live by the coast, and design forecasts are about opening the house to the outdoors.
- 85% of Australians live within 50 km of the coast (ABS; 2001 figure)
- Modern Coastal is a named 2026 style; stacker doors and "seamless indoor-outdoor connection" lead the forecasts (James Hardie / WGSN)
- Home Beautiful calls 2026 "the year of the outdoor room"

**Hero stat:** 85% – Australians who live near the coast
**The Implication:** *Architects:* specify corrosion-resistant, flush heat that disappears into the ceiling. *Homeowners:* coastal homes need finishes made for salt air.
**Bromic Insight:** Heat designed for coastal homes and recessed out of sight.
