## 01 · The Patio Is A Revenue Line
**Capacity · Covers · Spend**
- More than 81% of North American full-service operators offer outdoor dining; the patio is about 20% of sales (TouchBistro)
- 30% expanded patio capacity in the past year (TouchBistro)
- Canadian outdoor dining +24% year on year (OpenTable)

**Hero stat:** 1 in 5 dollars – the patio's average share of full-service sales
**The Implication:** *Operators:* staff, price and heat outdoor seats as core capacity. *Developers:* lease indoor plus terrace. *Designers:* heat, shade and power in the first drawings.
**Bromic Insight:** A heated outdoor seat is capacity you earn from in every season.

## 02 · Covered And Comfortable Wins
**Cover · Warm · Convert**
- 62% of Canadians choose a restaurant specifically for its outdoor seating (OpenTable, n=1,502)
- 25% have stayed outdoors in bad weather rather than move inside (OpenTable)
- Toast US survey (to confirm): 62% prefer a covered or screened patio; weather is the top reason not to eat outside

**Hero stat:** 62% choose a restaurant for its outdoor seating (OpenTable Canada, 2026)
**The Implication:** Weather is the reason guests go inside; cover plus heat keeps them out. *Designers:* specify roof, screens and recessed heat as one system.
**Bromic Insight:** A covered, heated terrace turns a weather risk into a reason to book.

## 03 · Permanent, Year-Round Outdoor Dining
**Permit · Winterise · Open**
- New York legalised year-round roadway dining (August 2026)
- England's pavement licences permanent since 2024; City of Sydney fees waived permanently; Toronto runs about 1,500 outdoor spaces
- Hong Kong cut outdoor seating approval from a median 12 months to about a month (April 2026)

**Hero stat:** 12 months – New York's outdoor dining season, by law
**The Implication:** *Operators:* budget for permit lead times (more than half of NYC applications took over 19 months – NYC Comptroller). *Designers:* code-ready, demountable systems with fixed heat.
**Bromic Insight:** Year-round permits need year-round comfort.

## 04 · Electric Heat Becomes The Licence To Operate
**Electrify · Enclose · Comply**
- France has banned heating open terraces since 2022; a heated Paris terrace must be covered, airtight and closed by rigid walls
- UK councils increasingly require infrared on pavement licences; Amsterdam permits facade-mounted heating
- New York's all-electric rule extends to new buildings of 7+ storeys from July 2027, upheld in court (June 2026)

**Hero stat:** 2022 – France bans heated open terraces; electric, enclosed and controlled is the direction of travel
**The Implication:** *Operators:* plan the move from gas and portables to fixed electric heat. *Developers:* size electrical service for outdoor heat at base build. *Designers:* design enclosures to local rules.
**Bromic Insight:** Fixed, controlled electric infrared heat is built for where regulation is going.

## 05 · Every Hour Of Heat Is Counted
**Zone · Dim · Report**
- US restaurant costs +36% since 2019; utilities up by double digits (National Restaurant Association)
- 35% of operators name utilities among their steepest cost increases (SilverChef survey)
- UK grid electricity carbon factor down 26% in the 2026 conversion set (DESNZ); large hotel groups stay in EU CSRD scope from FY2027

**Hero stat:** −26% – the carbon of each UK kWh in the 2026 factors (DESNZ)
**The Implication:** Heat is judged per seat-hour, in cost and carbon. *Operators:* zone, dim and time to covers. *Hotel groups:* electric heat on cleaner grids lowers reported emissions.
**Bromic Insight:** Zoned, dimmable heat on demand means paying only for the seats in use.

## 06 · The Golden Hour Moves Earlier, The Season Stretches
**Sunset · Shoulder · Winter**
- In GB, 5–7pm overtook 7–10pm for rate of sale for the first time in 2025 (NIQ)
- Dubai's record month was December 2025 (2.04m visitors); Riyadh Season runs October–February
- 74% of Canadians plan to dine outdoors at least monthly in warmer months (OpenTable)

**Hero stat:** 5–7pm – the new peak in UK hospitality (NIQ, 2025)
**The Implication:** The sunset hour and cool winter evenings are when heat earns most. *Designers:* design for comfort at sunset. *Gulf operators:* winter evenings are peak season.
**Bromic Insight:** Heat is what makes the hours after sunset pay.

## 07 · Big Moments, Outdoors
**Watch · Gather · Celebrate**
- GB managed venues +62.4% on England's World Cup knockout day (1 July 2026, NIQ)
- UK pubs and bars revenue +90% during England matches; 10pm–1am transactions +121% (Square)
- US host-city restaurants +40.6% orders on match days (SpotOn/Square)

**Hero stat:** +121% – late-night sales on match days (Square, 2026)
**The Implication:** Event nights run late and cool. *Operators:* heated outdoor screen space pays back on big days. *Designers:* plan sightlines, acoustics and heat together.
**Bromic Insight:** The best nights run past sunset; heat keeps the crowd outside.

## 08 · Outdoor Wellness: Heat, Water, Nature
**Warm · Restore · Reset**
- 54% of travellers are interested in heat-centred or hydro wellness for 2027 (Hilton/Morning Consult)
- Wellness tourism $982bn in 2025 (+11.4%), forecast $1.38trn by 2030 (Global Wellness Institute)
- 35% would book a hotel for its outdoor relaxation spaces (Hilton)

**Hero stat:** 54% want heat-led wellness in 2027 (Hilton 2027 Trends)
**The Implication:** Warmth is the wellness experience. *Hotels:* design warm microclimates around pools, spas and terraces. *Developers:* outdoor wellness is a revenue zone.
**Bromic Insight:** Radiant warmth is the most natural wellness amenity outdoors.

## 09 · Resorts And Giga-Projects Build The Terrace In
**Develop · Specify · Open**
- Middle East pipeline: 771 projects, 188,960 rooms (record); Saudi Arabia about 58% (Lodging Econometrics)
- Saudi Arabia ~123m tourists in 2025; Dubai 19.59m visitors, 80.7% occupancy
- Japan a record 42.68m international arrivals in 2025 (JNTO)

**Hero stat:** 188,960 rooms – the Middle East's record hotel pipeline
**The Implication:** New resorts trade on outdoor evenings. *Designers:* specify heat suited to coastal and harsh conditions at concept stage. *Developers:* outdoor F&B that works on winter nights.
**Bromic Insight:** Heat designed in at concept stage disappears into the architecture.

## 10 · Bigger Yachts, Living On Deck
**Deck · Beach Club · Marine**
- 201 superyachts over 30 m delivered in 2025; 612 in build (Fraser)
- Order book 1,093 yachts over 24 m, with average length at a record high (BOAT International)
- Beach clubs and fold-down sea terraces lead 2026 launches (qualitative)

**Hero stat:** Record average length – yachts are getting bigger, and so are their decks (BOAT International, 2026)
**The Implication:** *Shipyards and designers:* open decks and beach clubs need marine-grade, low-profile heat. *Charter operators:* more usable deck evenings each season.
**Bromic Insight:** Heat built for the sea extends every evening on deck.

**Sub-theme: smoke-free terraces.** Belgium bans smoking on hospitality terraces from 1 January 2027; UK pub gardens are exempt. Terraces become family and dining spaces where comfort keeps guests outside.
