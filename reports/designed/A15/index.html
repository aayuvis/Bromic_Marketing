Bringing It To Your Buyers

Let Buyers Feel It

The surest way to sell warmth is to let buyers feel it.

1

Show it lit.

A heated model-home patio at dusk tells the story better than any brochure.

2

Price the pre-wire, even when buyers decline it.

A rough-in at framing costs far less than opening a finished soffit later, and it lets buyers add heat after closing.

3

Lead with the moment, then the tier.

"Dinner outside in October" sells better than a wattage.

4

Keep controls in the price.

A heater becomes a finished option once its controller is included.

Your Planning Checklist
  • Choose which plans get the program and mark the patio on each.
  • Send plans to Bromic's design team for heater counts and positions per tier.
  • Add Good, Better and Best to the options sheet with confirmed prices.
  • Add the rough-in scope to the electrician's framing checklist (see the pre-wire guide).
  • Brief the design-centre team and install the Better tier in the model home.
  • Electrical work by a licensed electrician to NFPA 70 as adopted locally; confirm with the local building authority.6
