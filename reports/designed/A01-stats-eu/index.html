2024

Pavement Terraces Are Here To Stay In England

Pavement licences became permanent in England on 31 March 2024. Councils can grant them for up to two years, and heaters are named among the furniture a licence covers, alongside umbrellas and barriers. Conditions vary by council, so check the local licence before choosing a heater.

Source: Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government, pavement licences guidance, 2024

The Future Homes and Buildings Standards come into force on 24 March 2027, subject to transitional provisions, so that new homes are built with low-carbon heating and high levels of energy efficiency.

13

In France, Heat Belongs On An Enclosed Terrace

Since 31 March 2022, French law has banned heating café, bar and restaurant terraces on public land, whatever the heater. Terraces that are covered and closed on their sides by solid walls are exempt unless the local authority objects; private land is not covered.

Source: Décret n° 2022-452 du 30 mars 2022, as summarised by CCI Paris Île-de-France, 2022

07Questions And SourcesHow Europe Lives Outdoors 2027
Q1

How many people in the UK have a garden?

77% of British adults have access to a private garden, about 44 million people, according to the Horticultural Trades Association (2025).

Q2

What share of Europeans live in a house rather than a flat?

In the EU, 51.7% of people lived in a house in 2023. The share is 79% in the Netherlands and 77% in Belgium, while 61% of people in Germany live in a flat.

Q3

Was summer 2026 the hottest on record in the UK?

Provisionally, yes. The UK's mean summer temperature was 16.5°C, the highest since records began in 1884, according to the Met Office.

Q4

Can pubs and restaurants in England put heaters on a pavement terrace?

Heaters are named among the furniture a pavement licence covers, and licences have been permanent in England since 31 March 2024. Each council sets its own conditions.

Q5

Are terrace heaters banned in France?

Heating open terraces on public land has been banned since 31 March 2022, whatever the heater. Covered terraces closed on their sides by solid walls are exempt unless the local authority objects.

Q6

Where do these numbers come from?

Each figure comes from a named public source: the HTA, Eurostat, the Met Office, KNMI, the DWD, CBS, the CGA RSM tracker, the UK government, French law and Carbon Brief.
