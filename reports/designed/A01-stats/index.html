  1. Most new US homes now come with a patio, and the patio keeps growing.

  3. A new patio comes close to paying for itself at resale and scores near the top for homeowner joy.

06Questions And SourcesOutdoor Living Statistics 2026
Q1

What percentage of new homes have a patio?

60.5% of US single-family homes started in 2025 came with a patio, according to NAHB's analysis of Census data. The share was 61.8% in 2024.

Q2

How big is a typical new-home patio in the US?

Homes built in 2024 have a patio of about 320 square feet on average, according to NAHB's Builder Practices Survey. Regional averages run from under 200 to over 400 square feet.

Q3

Is outdoor living still a design trend in 2026?

It is. AIA's Q2 2025 survey found outdoor living spaces were still the most popular exterior feature, with a net 55% of architects reporting rising popularity, down from 60% a year earlier.

Q4

Is a new patio a good investment?

On resale value alone it comes close to break-even, with Realtors estimating that a new patio recovers about 95% of its cost. Homeowners also rate it 9.9 out of 10 for joy.

Q5

How much do Americans spend on home improvement each year?

Americans spend about $525 billion a year on improvements and repairs to owner-occupied homes, according to Harvard's Leading Indicator of Remodeling Activity, released October 2026.

Q6

Where do these numbers come from?

Each figure comes from a named public source: NAHB, the AIA, Harvard's Joint Center for Housing Studies, Houzz, and the NAR with the NALP.
