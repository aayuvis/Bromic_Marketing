Project File · Pink Hill Hotel01 / 03
The Project
01

The Space

Pink Hill is a new pub built for live music, with outdoor terraces under high ceilings, sections of operable roof and feature walls that give each room its character.

02

The Challenge

The venue wanted its terraces in use through the colder months without losing the atmosphere. Heaters had to suit ceilings of very different heights and never block the view of a feature wall.

03

The Solution

After reviewing the site plans, Bromic’s commercial sales team specified a mix: compact Eclipse Pendants under the high fixed ceiling, Eclipse Electric over the dining tables and high-output Tungsten Electric in the larger rooms and under the operable roof.
