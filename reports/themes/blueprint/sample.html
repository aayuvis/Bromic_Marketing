02 · SELECTION, DETAILS AND CONTROLS

Choosing The Right Heater For Each Space

Begin with the people who will use the space. A dining table calls for even warmth at seated head height, a bar rail for heat where guests stand, and a lounge for gentle warmth without glare.

GENERAL NOTES

  1. A space counts as outdoor when at least 30% of its perimeter is permanently open. Size for the open condition.
  2. Dedicated branch circuits per NEC Article 424: conductors not less than 125% of the heating load.
  3. Maintain the manufacturer's minimum clearances and NEC 424.13. Show them on the shop drawings; confirm on site.
  4. Wiring methods, boxes and devices suitable for damp or wet locations, as the location requires.
  5. Electrical work by an electrician licensed in the project's jurisdiction.
  6. Integrations: Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, Control4, Crestron, Lutron. Compatibility varies by system.

03 · COVERAGE PLAN, CHECKLIST AND NEXT STEP

Your Planning Checklist

  1. 01Space confirmed as outdoor (at least 30% of the perimeter permanently open).
  2. 02Heater series chosen by use: seated, standing, lounge; coastal exposure noted.
  3. 03Coverage plan (D4) drawn over the furniture layout and checked by Bromic's design team.
  4. 04Controls scheduled for every zone: controller type, wall switch, app, any integration.
  5. 05Mounting heights and clearances taken from the current installation manual for each model.
  6. 06Recess cavities and blocking shown on the architectural sections (D1).
  7. 07Circuits, voltage and disconnects coordinated with the electrical engineer.
  8. 08Energy code check: does the AHJ require occupancy or timer control for outdoor heat?
  9. 09Substitution approach (Option A or B) agreed with the Owner.
  10. 10Tungsten Coastal and Select only where Bromic confirms availability for the programme.
