Executive Summary

The Pergola Is Now The Outdoor Room

Homeowners are roofing their patios and wiring them for year-round use. The pergola is where that money goes, and heat is the feature that turns three seasons into twelve.

Key insights

  • Outdoor rooms lead. Architects' net score for outdoor living rooms rose from 40% to 51% in a year 1; shade structures in Houzz projects rose 15 points to 35% 2.
  • Dealers raise the same six issues: bulk on slim beams, rust by the coast, controls that don't talk to the pergola, visible wiring and junction boxes, colour, and price 21,23,24,25.
  • Pergola makers are choosing heater partners now. StruXure offers the competition's heaters on its own brackets; Renson builds its own 30,29.
  • Control is consolidating. Bond, the bridge behind StruXure's app and the competition's smart switches, joined the Somfy Group on 30 July 2026 7,8,9.

So what

  • Tungsten Series II answers the beam: about 14% narrower, a third lighter, one single post with the wiring inside it.
  • Coastal answers the sea: 316 marine-grade stainless under a corrosion-resistant coating.
  • Affinity answers the app: Bromic's own controls, direct to Alexa, Google, Control4, Crestron and Lutron, independent of any one bridge.
  • A clear ladder answers price: Classic good, Select better, Platinum best, and Eclipse for the centre beam: heat and light in one.

Action plan

  • Dealers: pre-wire every heater position at build time; quote heater, control and circuit together.
  • Builders: design beam heights for an 8 ft minimum heater mount.
  • Bromic: publish a bracket and colour kit for the top five US pergola systems in 2027.

Dealer Issue 3 · Smart Home

One App For The Roof And The Heat

Bond brings "a particularly complete ecosystem for the outdoor segment."

Bond, on joining the Somfy Group, 30 July 2026 7

What changed

  • Somfy motors most louvered roofs: StruXure, Renson, Equinox, Corradi and Louvretec run on Somfy RTS or io 32,33,34,36,38.
  • Bond joined the Somfy Group on 30 July 2026 and says it will stay brand-agnostic 7. Its bridge already runs StruXure's pergola app 8 and the competition's Wi-Fi switches 9.
  • So the bridge between pergola and heater now belongs to the shade company. Dealers will want a heater platform that stands on its own and plugs into the home system.

The Affinity answer

  • Bromic's own controls and app: zones, schedules and timers, dimming in 1% steps on the Dimmer.
  • Direct to voice: Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, no extra hub.
  • Into the home system: Control4, Crestron and Lutron; Ethernet and 0–10 V for building management on the 5-channel controller.
  • Sized for pergolas: 1, 2 or 5 channels, each up to 6 kW at 208–277 V; one Eclipse per channel on the Eclipse controller.

Dealer Issue 4 · Mounting And Install

One Post, Wiring Inside, Nothing On Show

"Mount heaters to main structural beams, not louver slats."

Hansø, pergola heater placement guide 22

What the pergola gives you

  • Hollow posts and beams are the wire chase: StruXure calls them "dedicated, hidden channels for all wiring" 32.
  • The post is often the downspout too 32,33,35: use wet-location wiring and keep connections out of the water path.
  • Fan beams and sub-beams are the mounting points pergola makers already provide 32,34. Never fix to a rotating louvre 22,23.

What Series II gives you

  • Single post in place of Series I's two tubes, with the cable inside it and no junction box on show.
  • Mounting bracket with set positions at 15°, 30° and 45° on a 2.5 × 4 in plate.
  • Wall bracket 14 in or 18 in reach; pitched adaptor 3/12 to 12/12; low-clearance recess kit for a 7.5 in cavity, fan-ventilated, box supplied.
  • Pre-wire at build: $200–$400 then saves $600–$1,200 later, by one dealer's figures 25.

Sources: launch deck 12, Series II manuals 15. Tilt on 4 and 6 kW: the deck says 45°, the Series II manual 30°; to confirm with the Product team.

Spacing and clearance

  • Between heaters: at least 20 in edge to edge (Tungsten) 17.
  • Above and beside: 12 in from the heater top to the ceiling and to side walls (electric) 18.
  • In front: combustibles at least 3 ft from the face 17.

  • Affinity Eclipse controller: one Eclipse a channel; or the Eclipse Dimmer Controller with remote.
  • Manual wall switch as the local disconnect point where the code asks for one.
  • Expansion board (built into 5-channel): Ethernet and 0–10 V for building management.

The circuit

  • Each 240 V heater or controller channel is hard-wired by a licensed electrician on a dedicated circuit; check panel space first 24,28.
  • Outdoor circuits feeding heaters: GFCI and permit rules vary by jurisdiction; one county requires GFCI protection inside the building 28. Ask the local inspector.
  • Pre-wire every heater position when the pergola goes up 25.

Gas under a pergola

  • Gas heaters cannot be recessed 18.
  • With a roof and more than two walls, at least 25% of the total wall area must be fully open, plus 30% of the rest 19.
  • Tungsten Gas: 3 ft to combustibles above, 14 in with the heat shield; 8 ft minimum height 19.
  • Under most louvered roofs, electric is simpler: no gas line, no flue, no ventilation sums.

One fire on record: two radiant heaters installed without clearance to a combustible patio ceiling 27.

Dealer Checklist

Sell The Heat With The Pergola

At the first meeting

  • Ask how late and how cold they want to sit outside.
  • Measure the seating area and the clear height under the beam.
  • Note sea, pool and frame colour.
  • Show the ladder: Classic, Select, Platinum, Eclipse.

At design

  • Mark heater positions on a fan beam or the perimeter beam, never a louvre.
  • Check 8 ft for ceiling heaters; post or wall mount below that.
  • Keep heaters clear of louvre motors and the blades' arc.
  • Pick the finish: black, white or graphite.

At build

  • Pre-wire each position through the hollow post and beam.
  • Dedicated circuits, sized by the electrician; GFCI and permit as the inspector requires.
  • Wet-location fittings where the post is the downspout.

At handover

  • Affinity on the customer's phone; zones and schedules set.
  • Voice or home-system link where they have one.
  • By the sea: show the fortnightly wipe-down.
