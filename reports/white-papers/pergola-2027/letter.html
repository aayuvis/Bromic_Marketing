Executive Summary

The Pergola Is Now The Outdoor Room

Homeowners are roofing their patios and wiring them for year-round use. The pergola is where that money goes, and heat is what turns three seasons into twelve.

Key insights

  • Outdoor rooms lead. Architects' net score for outdoor living rooms rose from 40% to 51% in a year 1; shade structures in Houzz projects rose 15 points to 35% 2.
  • Our market research shows six things pergola buyers want from heat: a slim look on slim beams, durability by the water, smart control that joins the rest of the outdoor room, clean installs, colours that match the frame, and an option for every budget 3.
  • Pergola makers are choosing heater partners now, and building heat into their own systems.
  • Control is converging: roofs, screens and lights already run from one app. Heat is the next piece, and it needs smart controls that integrate.

So what

  • Tungsten Series II answers the beam: about 14% narrower, a third lighter, one single post with the wiring inside it.
  • Coastal answers the sea: 316 marine-grade stainless under a corrosion-resistant coating.
  • Affinity answers the app: Bromic's own controls, with Alexa, Google, Control4, Crestron and Lutron.
  • A portfolio answers the budget: Tungsten Classic and Select give the same heat at two price points; Platinum is the architectural choice; Eclipse brings heat and light to the centre beam.

Action plan

  • Pergola makers: add a Bromic heat option and pre-wired heater positions to the standard spec.
  • Dealers: quote heater, control and circuit together, at build time.
  • Bromic: a Series II bracket and colour kit for the leading pergola systems in 2027.

What Our Research Shows

Six Things Pergola Buyers Want From Heat

We studied pergola makers' specifications, dealer and installer guides, and the questions that come up on real projects 3. Six themes stood out. Each is an opportunity, and Bromic has an answer for each.

01

Slim Structures Call For Slim Heat

Pergolas are designed around clean, slim lines. Buyers want heat that reads as part of the architecture, not an add-on.

Bromic: Tungsten Series II: about 14% narrower and a third lighter, on one single post.

02

Life By The Water

Many pergolas sit by the sea or a pool, where salt and chlorine test every metal on the structure.

Bromic: Tungsten Coastal: 316 marine-grade stainless under a corrosion-resistant coating. Platinum Marine for the most exposed sites.

03

One Connected Outdoor Room

Owners run the roof, lights and screens from their phone. They expect heat to join them, with smart controls that integrate.

Bromic: Affinity Smart-Heat and the Bromic app: zones, schedules, dimming, voice and home-system control.

04

Clean Installs, Nothing On Show

Pergola makers hide wiring inside posts and beams. Exposed conduit and junction boxes undo that work.

Bromic: A single post with the cable inside it and no junction box on show.

05

Colour That Matches The Frame

Frames come in black, white, grey and bronze. The heater should sit with the frame, not against it.

Bromic: Tungsten Select in black, white and graphite; Platinum in black or white glass.

High-performance heaters do not fit every pergola customer's budget, and the heater is often the first line cut.

Life By The Water

Built For Salt Air

Coastal and pool-side pergolas are where heaters are tested hardest. Pergola makers handle it in different ways: some limit finish warranties near the sea, others specify marine-grade stainless hardware. A heater under that roof should be built to the same standard.

Tungsten Coastal

  • Two layers: 316 marine-grade stainless steel under a high-temperature, corrosion-resistant coating.
  • Recess kit in powder-coated 316 stainless, IPX5.
  • 4 and 6 kW, 208 and 240 V, black or white.
  • For coastal homes and venues, not marine craft.

Platinum Electric Marine

  • AISI 316 stainless; IPX5 (IP55 on 4500 W).
  • Warranty 5 years on land-based installs.

Care by the coast

Stainless is not stain-free. Bromic's manuals ask for salt spray to be wiped off and a fortnightly clean by the coast, with a yearly check by a qualified technician 15,17.

Clean Installs

One Post, Wiring Inside, Nothing On Show

Pergola makers already route power through hollow posts and beams. Series II keeps it hidden all the way to the heater, and its accessories fit every surface a pergola offers.

Single post

Fixes to a beam or ceiling; the cable runs inside it. No junction box on show.

Wall bracket

For posts, beam faces and walls: 14 in or 18 in reach.

Angled mounting bracket

Set positions at 15°, 30° and 45° to aim the heat at the seats.

Low-clearance recess kit

Flush in a 7.5 in cavity, fan-ventilated, recess box supplied.

Pitched-ceiling adaptor

For gabled pergolas and pitched patio covers: six set holes from 3/12 to 12/12.

On the pergola

  • Fix to the main structure: the perimeter beam, a fan beam or a sub-beam. Never to a rotating louvre.
  • Run power inside the hollow post and beam; where the post is also the downspout, use wet-location wiring.
  • Pre-wire every heater position when the pergola goes up: it costs a fraction of a retrofit.

Sources: launch deck 14, Series II manuals 16. Tilt on 4 and 6 kW: the deck says 45°, the Series II manual 30°; to confirm with the Product team. Accessory images: Bromic renders of Series II.

Bromic recommended heights 22. Wall or post mounts can sit lower, from 6 ft, angled at the seats 19.

Where and how to mount

  • Perimeter beam: Tungsten on its single post, angled 15–45° in toward the seats.
  • Fan beam over the table: Eclipse pendant, or Tungsten centred over a long table.
  • Posts and walls: wall bracket where the clear height is below 8 ft, or to reach the edge seats.
  • Solid bays and pavilions: recess flush with the low-clearance kit.
  • Never on a louvre; keep clear of louvre motors and the blades' arc, and of fabric.
  • Spacing: at least 20 in between heaters; 12 in from the heater top to the ceiling; combustibles 3 ft from the face 15,21.

How many heaters

Heaters = seating area ÷ (heat area × cover factor × climate factor), rounded up: partly covered 1.0, cool evenings around 40–50°F 0.85 24. Tungsten Classic and Select share the same heat areas.

Controls on every quote

  • Affinity On/Off or Dimmer: 1, 2 or 5 channels, up to 6 kW a channel at 208–277 V.
  • Affinity Eclipse controller: one Eclipse a channel; or the Eclipse Dimmer Controller with remote.
  • Manual wall switch as the local disconnect where the code asks for one.

The circuit

  • Each heater or controller channel is hard-wired by a licensed electrician on a dedicated circuit; check panel space first 25.
  • GFCI and permit rules vary by jurisdiction. Ask the local inspector.
  • Pre-wire every heater position when the pergola goes up.

Gas under a pergola

  • Gas heaters cannot be recessed 21.
  • With a roof and more than two walls, at least 25% of the total wall area must be fully open, plus 30% of the rest 20.
  • Tungsten Gas: 3 ft to combustibles above, 14 in with the heat shield; 8 ft minimum height 20.
  • Under most louvered roofs, electric is simpler: no gas line, no flue, no ventilation sums.

For Pergola Makers And Dealers

Make Heat Part Of Every Pergola

What Bromic brings a pergola maker

  • A heat option for your spec: Classic, Select, Coastal, Platinum and Eclipse, in your frame colours.
  • CAD and Revit files for your drawings and configurators.
  • A light load: Series II weighs 13–15 lb on one 2.5 × 4 in plate.
  • IPX5 for heaters that see rain through open louvres.
  • Affinity for app and home-system control alongside your roof.
  • The Bromic Partner Program: partner pricing, design plans, extended warranty, one point of contact, project marketing.

Still to agree with each maker

  • Approved mounting points and fixings on their beams.
  • The frame warranty when a heater is fitted.
  • Where the heater circuit shares a post with the downspout.

Dealer checklist

  • Ask how late and how cold they want to sit outside.
  • Measure the seating area and the clear height under the beam.
  • Note sea, pool and frame colour.
  • Show the portfolio: Classic, Select, Platinum, Eclipse.
  • Mark heater positions on beams, never louvres.
  • Pre-wire each position through the post and beam.
  • Affinity on the customer's phone at handover; zones and schedules set.
  • By the sea: show the fortnightly wipe-down.

Bromic next steps

  • A Series II bracket and colour kit for the leading pergola systems.
  • Affinity drivers confirmed for Control4, Crestron and Lutron.
