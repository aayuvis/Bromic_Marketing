Three Questions To Ask Yourself

  1. 1
    Your SpaceIs it covered, partly covered or open? Count the seats: plan about 20 ft² a seat.
  2. 2
    MountingCeiling, wall, recessed flush, on a pole – or freestanding, so it moves with the table?
  3. 3
    Power Or FuelAn electric circuit at 208 V or 220–240 V, or a natural gas or propane line?

What Your Installer Will Need

Electric
  • A hard-wired circuit at 208 V or 220–240 V, sized by your electrician – or an outdoor outlet for Eclipse Freestanding.
  • A junction box at each heater: wall mounts at least 72 in above the floor, ceiling mounts at least 96 in (varies with output).
  • A place for the Affinity controller and a wall switch, with Wi-Fi there for the app.
  • For a flush ceiling, room for the recess kit – a 7.1 in cavity for Platinum Electric.

A circuit at 208 V or 220–240 V (an outdoor outlet for Eclipse Freestanding), a junction box at each heater, and a place for the Affinity controller and a wall switch.

Gas
  • A natural gas or propane line with an accessible shut-off.
  • Mounted gas heaters sit under cover at least 96 in above the floor and also need 110 V for ignition and controls.

A natural gas or propane line with a shut-off. Mounted gas heaters sit under cover, at least 96 in above the floor, with 110 V for ignition and controls.

Installation by a licensed electrician or gas fitter to local codes. Check every installation with your local building authority.
