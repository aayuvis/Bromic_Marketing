How To Sell It

When a customer names Infratech, start with the space, then the heater.

  1. 1

    Start With The Ceiling

    Ask where the heat will mount. Flat, pitched from 3/12 to 12/12, or recessed: Series II offers worry-free recessing up to 6,000 W, with a dual fan-ventilated kit and the recess box supplied. Infratech recesses 6 kW too; its flush frame needs a lined 2×8 pocket on a flat ceiling, and its drawing says: "After 4 hours of continuous use, a 15 minute cool down period is recommended".

  2. 2

    Show The Whole Heater

    Black, white or graphite carries through to the front, with three front patterns that swap without re-wiring. If the customer wants a specific colour, Infratech offers eight plus custom: say so, then show how the Bromic heater reads as one piece.

  3. 3

    Quote It With Its Controls

    Every heater goes on the quote with its Affinity Smart-Heat® control and a wall switch. Then the warranty: three years on the heater and seven on the elements.

Keep it fair

  • Factual lines only; never run down another brand.
  • Where Infratech leads or matches, say so: colours, voltages to 480 V, recessing to 6 kW.
  • Never "only" without its qualifier, and no superlatives about depth or clearance.
  • No prices, and no Infratech logo or imagery.

Confirm before quoting

  • Series II recess kit: 7.5 in as the cavity depth, no fire-rated lining under its listing, and continuous-use guidance
  • Tilt to 45° on 4 and 6 kW (the Series II manual gives 30°)
  • Coastal body material (316 per the Lookbook) and the coating's name
