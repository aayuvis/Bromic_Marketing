BROMIC VS INFRATECH · ON THE FLOOR

How To Talk About It On The Floor

When a customer names Infratech, start with the space, then the heater. Use these lines as written.

SIX FAIR LINESNO PRICES · NO DISPARAGEMENT
  1. 1

    THEY SAY"Infratech recesses too."

    YOU CAN SAYIt does, in a framed pocket of at least 2×8, lined on five sides with Type X drywall, on a flat ceiling. Tungsten Series II recesses 6,000 W into a 7.5 in ceiling cavity: the kit is dual fan-ventilated, so there is no site-built box.19,3

    Cavity depth and the kit's listing: To confirm.

  2. 2

    THEY SAY"Can a recessed heater run all evening?"

    YOU CAN SAYInfratech's flush-frame drawing says: "After 4 hours of continuous use, a 15 minute cool down period is recommended". The Series II kit is dual fan-ventilated.19,3

    Bromic's continuous-use guidance: To confirm. Promise no run time until it is.

  3. 3

    THEY SAY"My porch ceiling is pitched."

    YOU CAN SAYTungsten Series II mounts on 3/12 to 12/12 with its adaptor; Infratech's flush frame is "ONLY to be used on a flat non-sloped ceiling".2,19

  4. 4

    THEY SAY"Infratech comes in more colours."

    YOU CAN SAYIt does: brushed stainless or eight powder-coated colours plus custom, with the reflector and guard in stainless. Series II is all black, all white or all graphite, front included, in a Diamond, oval or triangle pattern.16,5

    Say so where Infratech leads.

  5. 5

    THEY SAY"What does the warranty cover?"

    YOU CAN SAYTungsten Series II: 3 years on the heater and 7 years on the elements. Infratech: a 3-year limited warranty, with a fourth year through its Elite Retailer and Installer programs.6,24

  6. 6

    THEY SAY"Which one is warmer?"

    YOU CAN SAYBoth reach 6 kW, and each brand measures coverage its own way. Talk about where the warmth lands, and let a free design plan prove the coverage seat by seat.2,29
