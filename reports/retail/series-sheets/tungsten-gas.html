The Series

Bromic’s gas workhorse: a compact mounted heater for covered patios, bars and homes with a gas line, and a wheeled freestanding tower for open terraces, sidewalks and pool decks. Every model runs on natural gas or propane.

Made For These Spaces
Restaurant terraces & barsPatios with a gas lineSidewalk cafésPool decks & lawns
01Warmth That Goes Where The Table Goes

The freestanding tower rolls on built-in wheels to wherever tonight’s seating is, and its tilting head aims the warmth at your guests.

02Compact Heat For Covered Spaces

On the wall, under the beam or on a drop pole, the mounted heater’s honeycomb face warms up to 215 ft² and stays out of the way.

03Natural Gas Or Propane, Every Model

Run it from the line you already have, or from propane where there is none: the tower houses a 20 lb cylinder in its base.

04Safety Built In

A flame failure device and an anti-tilt switch on the tower, and flame-monitored electronic ignition on the mounted heater.
