The Series

Eclipse pairs radiant heat with a warm, dimmable 3000K LED ring, so one fixture keeps the table warm and softly lit from sunset to the last glass. Hang it over the dining table, sweep it from a wall, or stand it beside the sofa on its own arch.

01Warmth And Light In One

Radiant heat and a dimmable LED ring in one fixture, with heat and light set separately from the remote or the app.

02Three Settings For Every Evening

Two elements give three heat steps, from 33% on a mild spring evening to the full 2,900 W on a cold night, over up to 121 ft² a heater.

03Seven Ways To Bring It In

The 24 in twin pole is included; 8, 24 and 48 in straight poles, a 24 in curved pole, a wall pole and the freestanding arch take it anywhere.

04Designed To Be Seen

A 27.6 in matte black disc with a radial ceramic screen and a halo of light: Eclipse is the heater you choose to show.
