The Series

Bromic’s design-led gas radiant heater: a ceramic-glass screen over a ceramic-tile burner, in black or stainless, for restaurant terraces, hotel and rooftop bars, pergolas and covered courtyards with a gas line.

Made For These Spaces
Restaurant terracesHotel & rooftop barsPergolasCovered courtyardsOutdoor kitchens
01Up To 39,800 BTU Of Radiant Warmth

Two sizes, 23,600 and 39,800 BTU, warm up to 160 and 215 ft², so a covered terrace stays full long after the sun goes down.

02Designed To Be Seen

A signature ceramic-glass screen in a black or stainless body turns the heater into part of the design.

03Natural Gas Or Propane, The Same Warmth

Factory-built natural gas and propane models carry the same rated input, so the fuel follows the site and the warmth stays the same.

04Engineered For Peace Of Mind

Electronic spark ignition watches the flame and locks out if it fails, on an ETL and cETL listed heater with a factory-fitted isolating valve.
