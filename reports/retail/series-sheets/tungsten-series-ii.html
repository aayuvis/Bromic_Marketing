The Series

Precise directional infrared heat in a slimmer body, now in three tiers. Classic brings honest warmth to the garage and workshop, Select dresses a pergola in your colour and front, and Coastal is built for life by the water – for coastal homes and venues, never on boats.

Made For These Spaces
PergolasCovered patiosVaulted porchesGarages & workshopsCoastal homes277 V commercial
01Up To 6 kW, Dimmed To The Evening

Dual elements on the 4 and 6 kW models, with the Affinity Smart-Heat® Dimmer to turn the warmth up on a cold night and down on a mild one.

02Flush In A 7.5 In Ceiling

Select and Coastal recess into a 7.5 in cavity. The kit is fan-ventilated and the recess box is supplied, so there is nothing to build on site.

03Visually Cleaner, Seemingly Floating

About 14% narrower and a third lighter than Series I, on a single post with no visible junction box.

04All Black, All White, All Graphite

Each finish covers the whole heater, with the Diamond front as standard and oval or triangle fronts sold separately on Select.
