On The Floor

Three Questions, The Right Heater

Ask them in order, and your customer's space points to the right series.

1

Is There A Roof, Wall Or Post?

  • No: Eclipse Smart-Heat® Freestanding for heat and light, or the Tungsten Smart-Heat® Freestanding Gas tower on propane or natural gas.
  • Yes: mount it, and go to question two. A space counts as outdoor when at least 30% of its perimeter is permanently open.
2

Electric Or Gas?

  • Electric dims with an Affinity Dimmer and can recess flush.
  • Gas is on or off, mounts under cover at least 8 ft up and needs 110 V for ignition: Tungsten Gas in matte black, or Platinum Gas in black or stainless.
3

How Should It Look?

  • Flush in a shallow ceiling: Tungsten Select or Coastal (7.5 in), or Platinum Electric (7.1 in).
  • On a pitched ceiling, a pergola, or up to 6 kW and 277 V: Tungsten Series II.
  • On show in a design-led room: Platinum Electric. Heat and light over a table: Eclipse.
  • By the sea: Tungsten Coastal or Platinum Electric Marine.

Then Size It

Allow 20 ft² a seat, aisles included, and divide by what one heater covers. A freestanding heater serves 4 seats at most. For anything beyond a simple patio, the design service draws the plan.

And Close With The Controls

Every Bromic heater is quoted with its Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls and a manual wall switch, on the same quote. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, sold separately.

Installation by a licensed electrician to NFPA 70 (NEC) or a licensed gas fitter to ANSI Z223.1/NFPA 54, and to the manufacturer's instructions; check every install with the local building authority.
