IN-STORE (SUGGESTED)

Display And Merchandising

Suggested ways to show Bromic in-store so customers feel the warmth and see the controls.

PHOTO Eclipse Smart-Heat® Electric over a dining table

Suggested

Let Them Feel It

  • A live electric heater mounted at its listed height, on an Affinity Dimmer, so customers feel warmth arrive in about 30 seconds and turn it up and down.
  • The Bromic app on a store tablet beside it, showing zones and schedules.
  • An Eclipse pendant over a set table, heat and light on together.

Suggested

Controls Beside Every Heater

  • Place the matching controller and wall switch next to each heater on display, with one shelf label covering both.

Suggested

Show The Choice

  • Tungsten Classic, Select and Coastal side by side as Good, Better, Best.
  • Finish samples in black, white and graphite, and the Diamond, oval and triangle fronts.
  • A sizing card at the counter (20 ft² a seat) and the design service number.
BEFORE YOU INSTALL A DEMO

Installation by a licensed electrician / gas fitter to local code. Display kits, fixtures and a showroom guide: what is available to retailers and how to order, To confirm

GOOD TO KNOW Quote every heater with its Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls and a manual wall switch. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, sold separately. Every idea on this page is a suggestion.
