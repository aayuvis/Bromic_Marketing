01 · The Range

One Family For Every Outdoor Room

Bromic covers every space your customers bring to the counter: electric heaters for ceilings, walls, posts and recesses, the Eclipse pendant with heat and light, Eclipse Smart-Heat® Freestanding, built-in gas, and the wheeled Tungsten Smart-Heat® Freestanding Gas tower on natural gas or propane.

02 · The Look

Designed To Be Seen

  • Tungsten Series II in all black, all white or all graphite, with a Diamond, oval or triangle front.
  • Platinum Smart-Heat® Electric behind a tinted glass-ceramic face, just 2.1 in deep.
  • Eclipse, a matte black disc with a dimmable 3000K LED ring.
  • Platinum Gas in black or stainless, behind a ceramic-glass screen.

03 · The Controls

Controls That Sell The Second Item

Every Bromic heater is paired with Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls: dimming in 1% steps on Platinum and Tungsten Electric, zones, schedules and timers in the Bromic app, with Alexa, Google Assistant, Control4, Crestron and Lutron where compatible.

Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, sold separately.
