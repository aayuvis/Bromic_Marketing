Sizing In 60 Seconds

Measure the seating area alone, at 20 ft² a seat with aisles included. Then divide by what one heater covers, adjusted for the roof and the weather:

THE ONE SUM

heaters = area ÷ (coverage × cover factor × climate factor), rounded up
