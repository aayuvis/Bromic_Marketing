Bromic vs Heatstrip · on the floor

How To Sell It

Start with the space, then the heater. Every heater goes on the quote with its controls.

Platinum Smart-Heat® Electric

Recommend it when

  • The heater should all but disappear: a dark glass-ceramic face, recessed flush or slim on a wall.
  • The customer asks which heater warms more of the table: Platinum is the one measured against Heatstrip.
  • The ceiling cavity is shallow: the low-clearance kit is fan-cooled. By the sea, the 316 Marine model.
Controls
Affinity Smart-Heat® Dimmer or On/Off, and a manual wall switch. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity controls, sold separately.

Tungsten Smart-Heat® Electric Series II

Recommend it when

  • The heater is part of the look: black, white or graphite, front included, with three front patterns.
  • The ceiling is pitched, or the heat needs a single post or a tilt toward the seats.
  • The customer wants the longest cover: 3 years on the heater and 7 on the elements. By the sea, Coastal.
Controls
Affinity Smart-Heat® Dimmer as standard, On/Off where dimming is not wanted, and a manual wall switch. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity controls, sold separately.
  1. 1

    “Everyone has a Heatstrip.”

    Then compare the warmth. In an independent thermal test, Platinum 3400W warmed 33% more area than Heatstrip's Classic and Enhance 3200W, and up to 1.8× the area that gets properly warm.

  2. 2

    “I want it in the ceiling, but there isn’t much room.”

    Bromic offers worry-free recessing up to 6 kW: Tungsten Series II's low-clearance kit is dual fan-ventilated with the box supplied, and Platinum's has a built-in cooling fan. A licensed electrician installs it to AS/NZS 3000.

  3. 3

    “The Heatstrip comes with a remote.”

    The Enhance does, and it is rated IPX5. Bromic heaters are quoted with Affinity Smart-Heat® controls chosen for the space: dimming, zones, schedules and the Bromic app.

Keep it fair

  • Name Heatstrip in words only: no logo, image or product shot.
  • Say where Heatstrip leads: the IP rating, and the Enhance's included remote and app.
  • Quote test figures with the test: Infrascan, 35 minutes, 2.0 m above the target.
  • Tungsten has no test against Heatstrip: compare its specifications, never its warmth.
  • No prices.
