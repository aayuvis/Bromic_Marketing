## The story so far

Search results for a Venue magazine feature describe the Headlands Hotel setting Platinum Smart-Heat Electric heaters flush into its eaves and ceilings, so that guests hardly notice them, each covering up to about 6 m². [^s1] We could not read the article itself.

Every Bromic installation is completed by Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, an On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller with a wall switch and the Bromic Smart-Heat app, so the warmth is always exactly where, and when, it is wanted. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls, sold separately.

## Why this story matters

Flush electric heat on a coastal hotel shows the Platinum design case without a word of sales copy; it suits architects and coastal venues alike.
