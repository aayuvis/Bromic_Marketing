## The setting

Set in Juneau's temperate rainforest, this showhome divides its outdoor space into living, dining and grilling zones. The clients chose electric over propane or natural gas, and the designer described outdoor heat as close to a necessity in Alaska. [^s1][^s2]

## Bringing in the warmth

A Platinum Electric heater, chosen for its durability, warms the open grilling zone, while matte-black wall-mounted Tungsten Electric heaters were chosen to read as part of the architecture.

The sources do not name the controls used here. Every Bromic installation is completed by Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, an On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller with a wall switch and the Bromic Smart-Heat app, so the warmth is always exactly where, and when, it is wanted. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls, sold separately.
