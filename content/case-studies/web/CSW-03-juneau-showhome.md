## The space

A showhome in Juneau's temperate rainforest with its outdoor space split into living, dining and grilling zones. The clients chose electric over propane or natural gas, and the designer described outdoor heat as close to a necessity in Alaska. [^s1][^s2]

## What was installed

A Platinum Electric heater over the open grilling zone, chosen for durability, and matte-black wall-mounted Tungsten Electric heaters chosen to read as part of the architecture.

**Controls.** None A complete Bromic installation runs on its controls: an Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller, a wall switch and the Bromic Smart-Heat app. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls, sold separately.

## Why it matters for sales

Cold, wet climate; electric chosen over gas; zoning by use. Bromic already holds the photography and the Dwell feature, so this is the fastest of these drafts to publish.
