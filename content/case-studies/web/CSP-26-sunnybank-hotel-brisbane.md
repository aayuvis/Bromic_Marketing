## The story so far

Australian Hotelier reports that the Sunnybank Hotel upgraded its outdoor dining space with canopy shade structures, picnic tables and festoon lights, and added heaters so guests can sit out on a cold winter's night. The owner chose Bromic heaters powered by solar panels on the venue's roof, saying electricity had become more favourable thanks to green energy deals and that portable gas heaters face pushback over worker safety and bottle storage. [^s1]

Every Bromic installation is completed by Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, an On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller with a wall switch and the Bromic Smart-Heat app, so the warmth is always exactly where, and when, it is wanted. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls, sold separately.

## Why this story matters

An operator's own case for electric heat over portable gas, with solar on the roof, is the electrification story pubs want to hear.
