## What the sources say

In Blainville, north of Montréal, Mughet planned a backyard as one outdoor room: a Station Grill summer kitchen with a Dekton counter, a Delta Heat barbecue, an Alfa wood-fired oven, a Heat & Glo wood fireplace, Phantom motorised screens and Bromic Tungsten heaters. [^s1]

Mughet's lesson from the job is the order of work: screens before heaters. Once the exposed side was closed, two Tungsten heaters were enough where the first calculation called for four. [^s1]

**Controls.** The sources do not name the controls. Every Bromic installation is completed by Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, an On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller with a wall switch and the Bromic Smart-Heat app, so the warmth is always exactly where, and when, it is wanted. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls, sold separately.
