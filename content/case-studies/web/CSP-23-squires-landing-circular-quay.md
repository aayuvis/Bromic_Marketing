## The story so far

The Squire's Landing opened in May 2018 in the Overseas Passenger Terminal, a restaurant and microbrewery from Mantle Group and Lion. [^s2] A search snippet of a Venue magazine feature says about 240 seats are kept warm by Platinum Smart-Heat gas heaters in the ground-floor undercover areas and on the Level 1 verandah, that Bromic was involved from the planning stage with a detailed heating plan, and that the venue would otherwise lose almost 30% of its seating footprint. [^s1] Mantle Group later used Bromic again at Babylon. [^s3]

Every Bromic installation is completed by Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, an On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller with a wall switch and the Bromic Smart-Heat app, so the warmth is always exactly where, and when, it is wanted. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls, sold separately.

## Why this story matters

A harbour-front landmark where heat was planned before opening, and a repeat customer; the seating figure is a ready revenue story once confirmed.
