## What the sources say

In Saint-Hippolyte, Mughet fitted out an open summer kitchen facing the lake: a Station Grill kitchen with a Dekton counter, a Delta Heat barbecue and a Kamado Joe smoker, warmed by Bromic Tungsten heaters. An architect and the design studio Les Coupables worked on the project. [^s1]

Mughet explains the choice: the space is open, it faces the lake, and the brief asked for heat spread along a long prep-and-dining zone rather than one statement piece above a table. The Tungsten sits in the structure and does its job. [^s1] Mughet's project list describes the same job as a wooden pavilion with a kitchen and lounge by a pool, so the setting needs confirming. [^s2]

**Controls.** The sources do not name the controls. Every Bromic installation is completed by Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, an On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller with a wall switch and the Bromic Smart-Heat app, so the warmth is always exactly where, and when, it is wanted. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls, sold separately.
