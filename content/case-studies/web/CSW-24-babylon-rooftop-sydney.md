## What the sources say

Babylon sits on the rooftop of Westfield Sydney, a Mantle Group restaurant and bar designed by Hogg & Lamb and built by Xenia. [^s2] Its outdoor terrace and alfresco dining areas are warmed by 34 Platinum Smart-Heat Gas heaters: 22 of the 300 size and 12 of the 500 size. Bromic worked with the architects on the heating, and had already worked with Mantle Group at Squire's Landing on Circular Quay. [^s1]

**Controls.** The sources do not name the controls. Every Bromic installation is completed by Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, an On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller with a wall switch and the Bromic Smart-Heat app, so the warmth is always exactly where, and when, it is wanted. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls, sold separately.
