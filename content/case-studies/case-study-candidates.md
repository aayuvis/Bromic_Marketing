Every project named here is one Bromic has already published[^s1][^s2]. Nothing on this page is cleared for new numbers or quotes:
each needs the template's consent checklist before it is rewritten.

## What the audit found (38 published case studies)

| Market | Count | Segments |
|---|---|---|
| US | 20 | Restaurants (most), hotels, homes, a workplace, an all-electric multifamily building |
| Australia | 6 | Hotels and pubs (Calile, Oran Park, Pink Hill), Barangaroo House, a bowling club, a rooftop bar |
| UK / Europe | 7 | London restaurants (HIDE, a multi-site restaurateur), Pangbourne barn, Hotel Skansen (Sweden), a German pool house |
| Marine / other | 5 | Ritz-Carlton Evrima, Silver Nova, M/Y Coral Ocean, M/Y Lady Lene; Clínica Las Condes (Chile) |

Three gaps run through them. **21 of 35 never name the controls** (including a 70-heater ship and a 32-heater clinic). Three
heat-plan images, the most useful asset a specifier can see, do not display (Stella's, M/Y Coral Ocean, Hotel Skansen). Few
carry a result with a number. Upgrading the best existing studies is faster than finding new customers.
