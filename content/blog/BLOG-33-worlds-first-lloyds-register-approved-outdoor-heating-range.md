Bromic's Platinum Smart-Heat Electric Marine is an outdoor heater approved by Lloyd's Register for use at sea, certified to its ENV5 environmental category.[^s2] Bromic says it was the first outdoor heating range to receive that approval.[^s2] It is built in AISI 316 marine-grade stainless steel, rated IPX5, and comes in 2300, 3400 and 4500 W for about 65 to 143 ft² of deck, run by Affinity controls.[^s1]

## What does Lloyd's Register approval mean for a deck heater?

Lloyd's Register is a classification society. Its type approval says a product has been tested against the conditions it will meet on board. For an open deck that means salt spray and rough seas.[^s3] For a yard, a naval architect or an owner's team, an approved product is one less item to justify at survey.

Bromic designed the Marine range for those conditions: a casing that limits internal movement so the heater keeps working in a swell, and a sealed ceramic glass face that spreads heat evenly with little visible light.[^s3] Radiant heat also suits decks because it warms people and surfaces rather than air, so a breeze does not carry it away as fast.[^s5] See [do infrared heaters work in wind](item:RES-CO-KB1).

## How is it mounted on a vessel?

On a wall, on a ceiling pole or recessed flush into the deckhead, where it almost disappears.[^s1][^s2] Both Platinum recess kits accept the Marine version: the standard [Recess Kit](https://www.bromic.com/product/platinum-electric-recess-kit/) and the [Low-Clearance Recess Kit](https://www.bromic.com/product/platinum-electric-low-clearance-recess-kit/) for shallow ceiling voids. Minimum heights on land are 72 in on a wall and 96 in on a ceiling;[^s1] the vessel's class rules and the yard set what applies on board.

## Which controls does it need?

Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off or Dimmer controllers, or a manual wall switch, sold separately.[^s1] Every installation needs one. The Dimmer sets output in steps for mild evenings; zones let the crew warm the dining table without heating an empty lounge. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity controls, sold separately,[^s4] and a building-management connection uses the Affinity expansion board. The 2020 post named a "wireless dimmer"; the Affinity range replaces it. See the [controls overview](https://www.bromic.com/controls/).

## Frequently asked

### Can Platinum Electric Marine be used on a coastal home?

Yes. The warranty is five years on land-based installations.[^s1] For coastal terraces it is the Platinum choice; see the [Marine product page](https://www.bromic.com/product/platinum-smart-heat-electric-marine/).

### Is it recessed or surface-mounted on yachts?

Usually recessed into the deckhead, though wall and ceiling-pole mounting are available.[^s2]

### What does ENV5 mean?

It is the Lloyd's Register environmental category the heater is certified to.[^s2] [[VERIFY: plain-language definition of ENV5 from the Lloyd's Register Test Specification Number 1 — Mike Clifford]]

### Are controls included?

No. Affinity controllers and the manual wall switch are sold separately.[^s1]
