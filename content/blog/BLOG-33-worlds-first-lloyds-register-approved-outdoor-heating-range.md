When the anchor drops and the sun slips below the horizon, Bromic's Platinum Smart-Heat Electric Marine keeps the deck warm enough to stay out for dinner. It is approved by Lloyd's Register for use at sea, certified to its ENV5 environmental category,[^s2] and Bromic says it was the first outdoor heating range to receive that approval.[^s2] Built in AISI 316 marine-grade stainless steel and rated IPX5, it comes in 2300, 3400 and 4500 W for about 65 to 143 ft² of deck, run by Affinity controls.[^s1]

## What Lloyd's Register Approval Means On Deck

Lloyd's Register is a classification society, and its type approval tells you a product has been tested against the conditions it will meet on board. For an open deck, those conditions are salt spray and rough seas.[^s3] For a yard, a naval architect or an owner's team, an approved heater is one less item to justify at survey.

We designed the Marine range with life at sea in mind. Its casing limits internal movement so the heater keeps working in a swell, and a sealed ceramic glass face spreads warmth evenly with very little visible light.[^s3] Radiant heat is also well suited to decks, because it warms people and surfaces rather than the air around them, so a passing breeze carries far less of it away.[^s5] You can read more in [do infrared heaters work in wind](item:RES-CO-KB1).

## Mounting That Disappears Into The Design

Platinum Electric Marine can sit on a wall, hang from a ceiling pole or recess flush into the deckhead, where it all but vanishes into the lines of the vessel.[^s1][^s2] Both Platinum recess kits accept the Marine version: the standard [Recess Kit](https://www.bromic.com/product/platinum-electric-recess-kit/) and the [Low-Clearance Recess Kit](https://www.bromic.com/product/platinum-electric-low-clearance-recess-kit/) for shallow ceiling voids. On land, the minimum heights are 72 in on a wall and 96 in on a ceiling;[^s1] on board, the vessel's class rules and the yard decide what applies.

## Warmth Exactly Where The Guests Gather

Every installation is paired with Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off or Dimmer controllers, or a manual wall switch, each sold separately.[^s1] The Dimmer steps the output down for milder evenings, and zones let the crew warm the dining table while an empty lounge stays off. Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity controls, sold separately,[^s4] and a connection to the building-management system uses the Affinity expansion board. The 2020 post mentioned a "wireless dimmer"; the Affinity range now takes its place. Explore the [controls overview](https://www.bromic.com/controls/) to see the full range.

## Frequently asked

### Can Platinum Electric Marine be used on a coastal home?

Yes. It carries a five-year warranty on land-based installations,[^s1] which makes it the natural Platinum choice for a terrace by the sea. Find out more on the [Marine product page](https://www.bromic.com/product/platinum-smart-heat-electric-marine/).

### Is it recessed or surface-mounted on yachts?

It is usually recessed into the deckhead for a seamless finish, though wall and ceiling-pole mounting are also available.[^s2]

### What does ENV5 mean?

ENV5 is the Lloyd's Register environmental category the heater is certified to.[^s2] [[VERIFY: plain-language definition of ENV5 from the Lloyd's Register Test Specification Number 1 — Product team]]

### Are controls included?

Affinity controllers and the manual wall switch are sold separately,[^s1] and every installation needs one of them.
