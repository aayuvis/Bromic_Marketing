In 2026, the amenities shaping US homes are outdoor rooms: a lounge, a dining table, an outdoor kitchen, often a covered structure and, more and more, a corner for wellness. Six in ten new single-family homes now begin with a patio, averaging about 320 ft²[^s1], and architects rank outdoor living first among special-function rooms[^s2]. Warmth is what lets these rooms welcome you on cool evenings, yet only 23% of homeowners plan for it at the start[^s5].

![Arched heater over lounge chairs on a terrace overlooking a valley at sunset](img:w-bromiceclipseheater-scottsdalehome-10)

## Outdoor Rooms That Mirror The Ones Inside

Homeowners are recreating the rooms they love indoors. In the 2026 U.S. Houzz Outdoor Trends Study, 83% of renovated outdoor spaces include a lounge or seating area[^s3], while dining areas appear in 55% and outdoor cooking areas in 48%[^s4].

| Amenity | How common (2026) | What keeps it in use after dark |
|---|---|---|
| Lounge or seating area | 83% of renovated outdoor spaces[^s3] | Radiant heat over the seating, plus lighting |
| Dining area | 55%[^s4] | Heat over the table, on a dimmer |
| Outdoor cooking area | 48%[^s4] | Heat at the prep and serving zone; plan power with the kitchen |
| Fireplace or fire pit | 48% of lounge areas[^s4] | Ambience; pair with radiant heat for the whole group |
| New-home patio | 60.5% of 2025 starts, about 320 ft² average[^s1] | A room-sized space that needs room-level services |

## The Outdoor Kitchen, And The Perfect Moment To Plan Warmth

When the outdoor kitchen goes in, the trades are already on site. In the Houzz study, grills appear in 85% of outdoor kitchens, and 68% of outdoor kitchen projects include infrastructure work such as plumbing, electrical or heating[^s4]. While the electrician is running circuits for refrigeration and lighting, a heater circuit and its switch cost the least to add.

## Covered Structures That Turn A Patio Into A Room

Pergolas, roofs, retractable covers and stacking glass doors transform a patio into a true room, and they give heaters a natural home as well. Designer Danny Wang made the point in the original version of this post, describing how a structure offers shade by day and mounting points for heaters at night[^s8].

Beneath a cover, a heater can be recessed or ceiling-mounted, discreetly out of sight. Platinum Smart-Heat Electric can be recessed, ceiling- or wall-mounted and covers approx. 65–143 ft² depending on model and installation[^s7].

## The Fire Pit And The Warmth Around It

A fire pit on its own rarely keeps everyone warm. It brings a focal point and atmosphere, and nearly half of outdoor lounges now include one[^s4], though its warmth reaches mainly the people nearest the flame. Radiant heat warms people and surfaces directly and holds up in a light wind[^s6], so a heater over the seating keeps the whole group comfortable. Most well-loved outdoor rooms have both.

## Wellness Moves Outdoors

Saunas, cold plunges and hot tubs are finding their way outside. They are enjoyed in cool weather by design, often at the start or end of the day, and warmth over the changing and seating area makes the moments between the plunge and the towel far more comfortable. Choose heaters and mounting positions with the moisture in mind, and check the product's rating and clearances with your installer.

## Frequently asked

### What is the most popular outdoor amenity in 2026?
A lounge or seating area, which Houzz found in 83% of renovated outdoor spaces[^s3].

### How many new homes have a patio?
About six in ten, or 60.5% of single-family homes started in 2025, according to NAHB's reading of Census data[^s1].

### When should outdoor heating be planned?
Plan it alongside the patio, kitchen and structure, so the circuit, mounting and controls go in once. Only 23% of homeowners prioritize heating at the planning stage[^s5].
