The amenities shaping US homes in 2026 are outdoor rooms: a lounge, a dining table, an outdoor kitchen, often a covered structure, and increasingly a wellness corner. Six in ten new single-family homes now start with a patio, averaging about 320 ft²[^s1], and architects rank outdoor living first among special-function rooms[^s2]. What decides whether these rooms get used on cool evenings is heat, and only 23% of homeowners plan for it at the start[^s5].

![Arched heater over lounge chairs on a terrace overlooking a valley at sunset](img:w-bromiceclipseheater-scottsdalehome-10)

## What are homeowners building outside?

Rooms that mirror the ones indoors. In the 2026 U.S. Houzz Outdoor Trends Study, 83% of renovated outdoor spaces include a lounge or seating area[^s3]. Dining areas appear in 55% and outdoor cooking areas in 48%[^s4].

| Amenity | How common (2026) | What keeps it in use after dark |
|---|---|---|
| Lounge or seating area | 83% of renovated outdoor spaces[^s3] | Radiant heat over the seating, plus lighting |
| Dining area | 55%[^s4] | Heat over the table, on a dimmer |
| Outdoor cooking area | 48%[^s4] | Heat at the prep and serving zone; plan power with the kitchen |
| Fireplace or fire pit | 48% of lounge areas[^s4] | Ambience; pair with radiant heat for the whole group |
| New-home patio | 60.5% of 2025 starts, about 320 ft² average[^s1] | A room-sized space that needs room-level services |

## Why are outdoor kitchens a heating decision?

Because the trades are already there. In the Houzz study, grills appear in 85% of outdoor kitchens, and 68% of outdoor kitchen projects include infrastructure work such as plumbing, electrical or heating[^s4]. When the electrician is running circuits for refrigeration and lighting, a heater circuit and its switch cost least to add.

## What role do covered structures play?

Pergolas, roofs, retractable covers and stacking glass doors turn a patio into a room. They also give heaters somewhere to go. Designer Danny Wang made the point on the original version of this post: a structure gives shade by day and mounting points for heaters at night[^s8].

Under a cover, a heater can be recessed or ceiling-mounted out of sight. Platinum Smart-Heat Electric can be recessed, ceiling- or wall-mounted and covers approx. 65–143 ft² depending on model and installation[^s7].

## Is a fire pit enough?

No. A fire pit gives a focal point and atmosphere, and nearly half of outdoor lounges now include one[^s4]. It warms the people nearest the flame. Radiant heat warms people and surfaces directly, and holds up in light wind[^s6], so a heater over the seating keeps everyone comfortable. Most well-used outdoor rooms have both.

## What about wellness spaces?

Saunas, cold plunges and hot tubs are moving outdoors. They are used in cool weather by design, often at either end of the day. Heat over the changing and seating area makes the time between the plunge and the towel comfortable. Choose heaters and mounting positions for the moisture involved, and check the product's rating and clearances with the installer.

## Frequently asked

### What is the most popular outdoor amenity in 2026?
A lounge or seating area. Houzz found one in 83% of renovated outdoor spaces[^s3].

### How many new homes have a patio?
About six in ten: 60.5% of single-family homes started in 2025, according to NAHB's reading of Census data[^s1].
