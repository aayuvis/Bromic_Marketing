Yes. Bromic's Eclipse is an electric radiant heater with a dimmable LED ring around its face, so one fixture warms and lights the same table. Each Eclipse heats about 121 ft², at 33, 66 or 100% output.[^s1] It comes as Eclipse Electric, mounted on a wall or ceiling pole, or Eclipse Freestanding, which rolls where it is needed. Both run from an Eclipse controller and can be switched at the wall.[^s2][^s3]

![Eclipse heater glowing inside its LED halo ring at dusk](img:w-bromic-eclipse-pendant-light)

## What makes Eclipse different from other heaters?

Most outdoor heaters do one job. Eclipse does two. The round ceramic glass face radiates heat to the people beneath it; the LED ring around it gives warm light that can be dimmed.[^s1] In summer the light works on its own, so the fixture earns its place all year.

Two fixtures become one, which matters on a small terrace or a busy ceiling: one pole, one cable, one thing for the eye to rest on.

## How is Eclipse Electric mounted?

On a pole from the ceiling or a wall, at least 96 in above the floor.[^s2] A 24 in twin pole comes as standard. Optional poles change the drop and the look:[^s2]

- [8 in straight pole](https://www.bromic.com/product/eclipse-8-inch-straight-pole/), for low ceilings
- [24 in straight pole](https://www.bromic.com/product/eclipse-24-inch-straight-pole/)
- [48 in straight pole](https://www.bromic.com/product/eclipse-48-inch-straight-pole/), for high or vaulted ceilings
- [curved pole](https://www.bromic.com/product/eclipse-curved-pole/)
- [wall-mount pole](https://www.bromic.com/product/eclipse-wall-mount-pole/)

The 2023 version of this post counted "seven" options; the collection page counts the freestanding model as one of them. Listing the poles is clearer than a number. [[VERIFY: Eclipse mounting option count (six vs seven) — Mike Clifford]]

## Which controller runs the heat and the light?

Every Eclipse needs a controller; it is part of the installation, not an add-on.

- **Affinity Smart-Heat Eclipse Controller.** Sets heat at 0, 33, 66 or 100% and dims the light, by zone, and connects to the Bromic Smart-Heat app for schedules and timers.[^s2][^s4]
- **Eclipse Dimmer Controller with remote.** Heat steps and light dimming from a handheld remote. It comes with Eclipse Freestanding.[^s3]
- **Manual wall switch.** Simple on and off at the wall, for Eclipse Electric.[^s2]

Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls, sold separately.[^s4] The 2023 post named an Eclipse Master Remote; the Affinity range now does that job. [[VERIFY: whether Eclipse Freestanding's included controller gives app control without an Affinity controller — Mike Clifford]] For setup, see our [controls guide](item:RES-BY-01) and the [controls overview](https://www.bromic.com/controls/).

## How many Eclipse heaters does a space need?

Start with about one heater per 121 ft² of seating, then adjust for wind, ceiling height and how open the space is.[^s1] For placement, see [where to place patio heaters](item:RES-PP-01). Bromic's [design service](https://www.bromic.com/design-your-space/) will draw a free heat layout from a photo or plan, and the [Eclipse collection](https://www.bromic.com/eclipse-collection/) page shows both models in place.

## Frequently asked

### Can the Eclipse light be used without the heat?

Yes. Heat and light are set separately, so the light can run on its own on summer evenings.[^s1][^s2]

### How much area does one Eclipse heat?

About 121 ft².[^s1]

### Does Eclipse run on gas?

No. Both Eclipse models are electric.[^s2]

### Can Eclipse Freestanding be moved?

Yes. It has a weighted base and integrated wheels.[^s1]
