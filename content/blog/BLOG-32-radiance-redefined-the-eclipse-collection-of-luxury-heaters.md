Yes. Bromic's Eclipse is an electric radiant heater with a dimmable LED ring around its face, so a single fixture warms and lights the same table as the evening draws in. Each Eclipse heats about 121 ft², at 33, 66 or 100% output.[^s1] It comes as Eclipse Electric, mounted on a wall or ceiling pole, or as Eclipse Freestanding, which rolls to wherever it is needed. Both run from an Eclipse controller and can be switched at the wall.[^s2][^s3]

![Eclipse heater glowing inside its LED halo ring at dusk](img:w-bromic-eclipse-pendant-light)

## Warmth And Light, Beautifully Combined

Where most outdoor heaters do a single job, Eclipse brings warmth and light together. Its round ceramic glass face radiates heat to the people gathered beneath it, while the LED ring around it casts a warm glow that can be dimmed to suit the mood.[^s1] On summer evenings the light works on its own, so the fixture earns its place all year round.

Bringing two fixtures together into one makes a real difference on a small terrace or a busy ceiling, with a single pole and cable and one graceful form for the eye to rest on.

## Mounting Eclipse Electric

Eclipse Electric hangs on a pole from the ceiling or a wall, at least 96 in above the floor.[^s2] A 24 in twin pole comes as standard, and optional poles let you tailor the drop and the look to your space:[^s2]

- [8 in straight pole](https://www.bromic.com/product/eclipse-8-inch-straight-pole/), for low ceilings
- [24 in straight pole](https://www.bromic.com/product/eclipse-24-inch-straight-pole/)
- [48 in straight pole](https://www.bromic.com/product/eclipse-48-inch-straight-pole/), for high or vaulted ceilings
- [curved pole](https://www.bromic.com/product/eclipse-curved-pole/)
- [wall-mount pole](https://www.bromic.com/product/eclipse-wall-mount-pole/)

The 2023 version of this post counted "seven" options, and the collection page counts the freestanding model as one of them. Listing the poles themselves is clearer than giving a number. [[VERIFY: Eclipse mounting option count (six vs seven) — Product team]]

## The Controller That Runs The Heat And The Light

Every Eclipse is paired with a controller, planned into the installation alongside the heater itself.

- **Affinity Smart-Heat Eclipse Controller.** Sets the heat at 0, 33, 66 or 100% and dims the light, by zone, and connects to the Bromic Smart-Heat app for schedules and timers.[^s2][^s4]
- **Eclipse Dimmer Controller with remote.** Heat steps and light dimming from a handheld remote, included with Eclipse Freestanding.[^s3]
- **Manual wall switch.** Simple on and off at the wall, for Eclipse Electric.[^s2]

Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls, sold separately.[^s4] The 2023 post named an Eclipse Master Remote, a role the Affinity range now fills. [[VERIFY: whether Eclipse Freestanding's included controller gives app control without an Affinity controller — Product team]] For setup, see our [controls guide](item:RES-BY-01) and the [controls overview](https://www.bromic.com/controls/).

## How Many Eclipse Heaters Your Space Needs

Begin with about one heater per 121 ft² of seating, then adjust for wind, ceiling height and how open the space is.[^s1] For placement, see [where to place patio heaters](item:RES-PP-01). ## Frequently asked

### Can the Eclipse light be used without the heat?

Yes. Heat and light are set separately, so the light can glow on its own on summer evenings.[^s1][^s2]

### How much area does one Eclipse heat?

About 121 ft².[^s1]

### Does Eclipse run on gas?

No. Both Eclipse models are electric.[^s2]

### Can Eclipse Freestanding be moved?

Yes. It has a weighted base and integrated wheels, so it can follow the furniture wherever you gather.[^s1]
