A patio feels like a resort when it is warm where you sit, softly lit after dark, furnished to last and arranged around something beautiful to look at. Begin with heat you can feel, not see: radiant warmth over the lounge or dining table, so the evening carries on long after the sun goes down. From there, layer the light, choose weatherproof materials, give the space a focal point and bring it all together under one control.

![Arched freestanding Eclipse heater over a lounge on a covered terrace that opens to the kitchen](img:w-bromic-eclipse-heater-scottsdale-home-6)

## 1. Bring Warmth To Where People Sit

The finest resorts direct their warmth to the seats where guests relax. Radiant heat warms people and objects rather than air, which is why it works so well outdoors, and under a partial cover it also builds a gentle pocket of warmth.[^s5] Place the heat directly over the table or sofa, where everyone will feel it, rather than at the edge of the space.

Where there is no wall or ceiling to mount on, the Eclipse Smart-Heat Freestanding Electric reaches over a table on a curved arm from a weighted, wheeled base, covering about 121 ft².[^s1] Where there is a soffit, a fixed heater on a pole or in the ceiling keeps the floor beautifully clear. Our [complimentary design service](https://www.bromic.com/design-your-space/) works out how many heaters you need and where each one belongs.

## 2. Layer The Light

Use three kinds of light: ambient light to see by, task light where you cook or pour, and accent light on plants, water or texture. The Eclipse heater adds a dimmable light around its rim, so a single fixture brings both warmth and a soft glow to the table.[^s1]

| Layer | What it does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Ambient | Lets you see the whole space | Downlights, wall lights |
| Task | Light to work by | Over the grill or bar |
| Accent | Gives depth | Uplit planting, lit water |
| Heat-light | Warmth and glow in one | Eclipse rim lighting, dimmable |

## 3. Choose Furnishings Made To Last

Resort patios are furnished with deep seating, a mix of materials and pieces that weather the seasons gracefully. Look to stainless steel, teak and weatherproof fabrics, layer in outdoor rugs, cushions and throws, and use large planters to separate dining from lounging. A bar cart or an outdoor kitchen makes the space a pleasure to host in.

## 4. Give The Space A Focal Point

A fire feature, water, a piece of art or a striking heater gives people a natural place to gather. The Eclipse's arched silhouette is often the first thing guests notice. At [Christopher's at Wrigley Mansion](https://www.bromic.com/case-study/christophers-at-wrigley-mansion/) in Phoenix, architect Wendell Burnette chose Eclipse freestanding heaters so the restaurant could add a patio while preserving the historic building's design, and in the live post he describes guests remarking on them.[^s4] [[VERIFY: quote wording against the case study before any direct quote is used — Marketing]]

## 5. Design Around The Way You Live Outdoors

Dining, lounging and cooking each deserve their own warmth and light. Zone the heat to match, so only the part of the patio you are using is on. Freestanding heaters move with the furniture, while fixed heaters suit zones that stay in place. Read more on [where to place patio heaters](item:RES-PP-01).

## 6. Set The Mood With A Single Touch

True luxury means the mood is set from your seat. The Eclipse Freestanding ships with the Eclipse Dimmer Controller and remote, which step the heat through 33, 66 and 100% and dim the light without you having to get up.[^s1][^s2] Fixed Platinum or Tungsten Electric heaters run on an Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off or Dimmer controller and a wall switch. Zones, schedules and voice control come through the Bromic Smart-Heat app; Wi-Fi and app features require compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately.[^s1] To get started, see [Smart-Heat controls](item:RES-BY-01).

## Frequently asked

### What makes a patio feel luxurious?

Comfort you hardly notice: warmth where you sit, soft layered light, beautiful materials and one striking focal point.

### Can a patio heater be a design feature?

Yes. A heater that also lights the table, like the Eclipse, can become the focal point of the space and a piece you are proud to show.

### Do I need a wall or ceiling for a heater?

No. A freestanding heater stands on its own base and needs only power and at least 79 in. of clearance above.[^s2]
