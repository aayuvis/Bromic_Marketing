Yes. Infrared outdoor heaters are safe when the type suits the space, the unit is certified, it is installed to code and to its manual, and anything that can burn is kept clear. Electric infrared burns no fuel, so it produces no carbon monoxide and suits covered patios. Gas infrared needs open air. Mount heaters at Bromic's minimum heights, and never leave one running unattended.[^s1][^s3]

![Three white Tungsten Electric heaters recessed in a beadboard porch ceiling above wicker seating](img:w-tungsten-electric-white-recessed)

## How does infrared heat work?

An infrared heater warms people, furniture and floors directly, the way sunlight does, rather than heating the air.[^s1] That is why it works outdoors, where warm air blows away. The element runs hot, so the safety questions are about placement, fuel and distance, not about the heat itself.

## Electric or gas: which is safer where?

| | Electric infrared | Gas infrared (natural gas or propane) |
|---|---|---|
| Combustion and carbon monoxide | None | Burns fuel; produces carbon monoxide[^s4] |
| Covered patio, pergola, porch | Suits it, at the mounting heights below | Only where the space stays open to fresh air |
| Garage, closed room, tent | Only a model and installation the manual allows | Never[^s4] |
| Code (US) | ANSI/NFPA 70, the National Electrical Code[^s1] | ANSI Z223.1/NFPA 54; propane cylinders also NFPA 58[^s1][^s6] |
| Installer | Licensed electrician | Licensed gas fitter |

## How open does a space need to be for gas?

Bromic's Planning Guide treats an area as outdoor when at least 30% of its perimeter is permanently open.[^s1] Two older Bromic posts gave different figures, 25% open wall space and 75% open walls.[^s3] Both are withdrawn here. The rule that governs your patio is the heater's installation manual and NFPA 54 as your local authority applies it. ## Do infrared heaters give off fumes or UV?

Electric models give off no fumes. Gas models produce carbon monoxide, which you cannot see or smell, so they belong in open air.[^s4] Infrared sits at longer wavelengths than visible light; ultraviolet sits at shorter ones. Bromic heaters are designed to emit infrared. [[VERIFY: any UV emission data for Bromic elements before claiming "no UV" — Mike Clifford]]

## Everyday safety checklist

1. **Certified and professionally installed.** Look for an Intertek, UL or CSA mark.[^s1]
2. **Level and stable.** Stand freestanding heaters on firm, level ground. Tungsten Freestanding has an anti-tilt design; Eclipse Freestanding has a weighted base.[^s7]
3. **Never unattended, never overnight.** Turn heaters off when people leave.[^s5] For gas, close the supply completely.[^s3]
4. **Propane cylinders.** Keep them upright and closed. Store them outdoors, ventilated, away from heat and anything that burns, and never in the house.[^s3][^s6]
5. **Leak test.** Brush soapy water on gas connections; bubbles mean a leak. Turn the gas off and call a gas fitter.[^s3]
6. **Keep it clean.** Clear burner and air passages on gas models; keep electric faces free of debris.[^s3] Book an annual service by a qualified professional.[^s3]
7. **Kids and pets.** Keep a 3-foot kid-free zone around any freestanding heater.[^s5] See [safety around kids and pets](item:RES-CO-KB2).

## Controls are part of safety

Every system runs from a control: an [Affinity Smart-Heat](https://www.bromic.com/controls/) On/Off or Dimmer controller, the Eclipse controller for Eclipse, or a manual wall switch.[^s7] Schedules and timers in the Smart-Heat app switch heat off when the evening ends; Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity controls, sold separately.[^s7]

Codes cover placement, supply, ventilation and clearance.[^s1] Confirm every installation with your local building authority and a licensed electrician or gas fitter, and read the [installation manual](https://www.bromic.com/technical-resources/) for your model.

## Frequently asked

### Can infrared patio heaters be left on all night?

No. Turn heaters off when you leave or go to bed.[^s5] An app schedule can do it for you with compatible Affinity controls.[^s7]

### Is an electric infrared heater safe under a covered patio?

Yes, mounted at or above Bromic's minimum heights (72 in on a wall, 96 in on a ceiling for Tungsten and Platinum Electric) and installed to code.[^s1] See [heaters under a covered patio](item:RES-PP-KB1).

### Can I use a gas patio heater in an enclosed space?

No. Fuel-burning heaters produce carbon monoxide and belong in open air.[^s4]

### How far should a patio heater be from combustibles?

At least 3 feet as a general rule, and whatever the model's manual specifies if that is more.[^s5]
