Yes – infrared outdoor heaters are safe when the type suits your space, the unit is certified, it is installed to code and to its manual, and anything that can burn is kept well clear. Electric infrared burns no fuel, so it produces no carbon monoxide and is at home on covered patios, while gas infrared needs open air. Mount every heater at Bromic's minimum heights, and never leave one running unattended.[^s1][^s3]

## How Infrared Warmth Reaches You

An infrared heater warms people, furniture and floors directly, much as sunlight does, instead of heating the air.[^s1] That is why it works so well outdoors, where warm air simply blows away. The element runs hot, so safety comes down to placement, fuel and distance.

## Electric Or Gas, And Where Each Belongs

| | Electric infrared | Gas infrared (natural gas or propane) |
|---|---|---|
| Combustion and carbon monoxide | None | Burns fuel; produces carbon monoxide[^s4] |
| Covered patio, pergola, porch | Suits it, at the mounting heights below | Only where the space stays open to fresh air |
| Garage, closed room, tent | Only a model and installation the manual allows | Never[^s4] |
| Code (US) | ANSI/NFPA 70, the National Electrical Code[^s1] | ANSI Z223.1/NFPA 54; propane cylinders also NFPA 58[^s1][^s6] |
| Installer | Licensed electrician | Licensed gas fitter |

## How Open A Space Needs To Be For Gas

Bromic's Planning Guide treats an area as outdoor when at least 30% of its perimeter is permanently open.[^s1] Two older Bromic posts gave different figures, 25% open wall space and 75% open walls,[^s3] and both are withdrawn here. The rule that governs your patio is the heater's installation manual, together with NFPA 54 as your local authority applies it. ## Fumes, UV And What Infrared Gives Off

Electric models give off no fumes. Gas models produce carbon monoxide, which you cannot see or smell, so they belong in the open air.[^s4] Infrared sits at longer wavelengths than visible light, and ultraviolet at shorter ones. Bromic heaters are designed to emit infrared. [[VERIFY: any UV emission data for Bromic elements before claiming "no UV" — Product team]]

## Your Everyday Safety Checklist

1. **Certified and professionally installed.** Look for an Intertek, UL or CSA mark.[^s1]
2. **Level and stable.** Stand freestanding heaters on firm, level ground. Tungsten Freestanding has an anti-tilt design, and Eclipse Freestanding a weighted base.[^s7]
3. **Never unattended, never overnight.** Turn heaters off when people leave.[^s5] For gas, close the supply completely.[^s3]
4. **Propane cylinders.** Keep them upright and closed, and store them outdoors in a ventilated spot, away from heat and anything that burns – never in the house.[^s3][^s6]
5. **Leak test.** Brush soapy water on gas connections, as bubbles mean a leak. If you find one, turn the gas off and call a gas fitter.[^s3]
6. **Keep it clean.** Clear the burner and air passages on gas models, and keep electric faces free of debris.[^s3] Book an annual service by a qualified professional.[^s3]
7. **Kids and pets.** Keep a 3-foot kid-free zone around any freestanding heater.[^s5] See [safety around kids and pets](item:RES-CO-KB2).

## Controls, Part Of Every Safe Installation

Every system runs from its control – an [Affinity Smart-Heat](https://www.bromic.com/controls/) On/Off or Dimmer controller, the Eclipse controller for Eclipse, or a manual wall switch.[^s7] Schedules and timers in the Smart-Heat app switch the warmth off when the evening ends, and Wi-Fi and app features need compatible Affinity controls, sold separately.[^s7]

Codes cover placement, supply, ventilation and clearance.[^s1] Confirm every installation with your local building authority and a licensed electrician or gas fitter, and read the [installation manual](https://www.bromic.com/technical-resources/) for your model.

## Frequently Asked

### Can infrared patio heaters be left on all night?

No. Turn heaters off when you leave or go to bed.[^s5] With compatible Affinity controls, an app schedule can do it for you.[^s7]

### Is an electric infrared heater safe under a covered patio?

Yes, mounted at or above Bromic's minimum heights (72 in on a wall, 96 in on a ceiling for Tungsten and Platinum Electric) and installed to code.[^s1] See [heaters under a covered patio](item:RES-PP-KB1).

### Can I use a gas patio heater in an enclosed space?

No. Fuel-burning heaters produce carbon monoxide and belong in the open air.[^s4]

### How far should a patio heater be from combustibles?

At least 3 feet as a general rule, or whatever the model's manual specifies if that is more.[^s5]
