Yes. Radiant outdoor heaters work in winter because they do not try to warm the cold air. They send infrared heat straight to people, furniture and floors, the way low winter sun still warms your face on a cold day[^s1]. Cold air and wind take that warmth from your skin faster, so a winter patio needs the right heater, aimed at the seating, with shelter from the wind and a cover overhead where you can.

![Screened porch with a vaulted timber ceiling, a fireplace and two black heaters on drop poles](img:w-dsc05737-courtney-saib)

## Why do radiant heaters still work in the cold?

A heater that warms air has no chance outside in winter: the warm air rises and leaves, and any breeze takes it sooner[^s1]. A radiant heater skips the air. Its heat is absorbed by whatever it reaches, so you feel it even when the air around you stays cold[^s1].

What the cold does change is how quickly you lose that warmth. Moving air strips heat from exposed skin, which is why it feels colder than the thermometer says[^s3]. The heater keeps delivering; the space and the plan decide whether it keeps up.

| Winter factor | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Low air temperature | Skin loses heat faster | Plan more coverage over seating; sit inside the heat pattern |
| Wind | Carries heat from skin and disperses warm air[^s3] | Screen the windward side; choose the most radiant heater |
| Open sky | Nothing holds warm air overhead | Add a roof or soffit to trap a heat cloud[^s1] |
| Damp or coastal air | Harder on finishes and fixings | Choose materials and water ratings for the site |

## Which heaters cope best with winter?

The most radiant ones. Short wave is about 90% radiant and warms instantly; long wave is about 40% radiant, takes around five minutes and is the most easily dispersed by wind[^s2]. On a cold evening that difference is the whole result. The bands are explained in [infrared vs radiant heaters](https://www.bromic.com/blog/what-is-the-difference-between-an-infrared-heater-and-a-radiant-heater/).

## Five things that make a winter patio work

**1. Put a roof over it.** A soffit, roof or closed pergola holds a layer of warm air, the heat cloud, which adds to the radiant heat below. Keep at least 30% of the perimeter permanently open so it remains an outdoor space[^s1].

**2. Block the wind.** Glass screens, retractable side blinds, walls and planting on the windward side all help. Wind direction is one of the first things Bromic's planning guide asks about[^s1]. See [do infrared heaters work outdoors](https://www.bromic.com/blog/do-infrared-heaters-work-outdoors/) for wind.

**3. Heat the seats, not the floor.** Aim directional heaters at where people sit and overlap coverage where they stay longest. Coverage varies with model, mounting height, environment and installation[^s5], so let Bromic's complimentary [Design Your Space](https://www.bromic.com/design-your-space/) service draw the layout.

**4. Choose for the site.** Near the coast, Platinum Smart-Heat Electric Marine is built from 316 marine-grade stainless steel and rated IPX5[^s6]. For other models and wet weather, see [patio heaters in the rain](https://www.bromic.com/blog/patio-heaters-in-the-rain-what-you-need-to-know/).

**5. Plan it before the cold.** Only 23% of homeowners rank outdoor heating a top priority when they plan an outdoor space[^s4]. ## Frequently asked

### Can I use an outdoor heater in snow?
Heaters rated for outdoor use can run in winter weather, but follow the model's manual on clearances, covers and water rating, and keep snow off and away from the heater. Care tips are in [cleaning and maintenance for outdoor heaters](https://www.bromic.com/blog/keeping-the-heat-on-essential-cleaning-and-maintenance-for-outdoor-heaters/).

### Can a thermostat keep my patio at a set temperature?
No. Outdoor air cannot be held at a set point[^s1]. Control the heater's output with an Affinity controller instead.

### How many heaters do I need for winter?
More than for a mild evening on the same patio, because exposure and cold raise the heat needed. Start from coverage per heater and let a layout confirm it.

### Will a fire pit do the same job?
A fire pit warms the people closest to it. Overhead radiant heaters cover the whole seating area; many patios use both.
