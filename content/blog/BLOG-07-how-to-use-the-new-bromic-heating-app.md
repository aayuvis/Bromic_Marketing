The Bromic heating app, listed in the stores as Bromic Smart-Heat, runs Bromic heaters from a phone once compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls are installed.[^s1] You pair the controller by Bluetooth within about 30 ft, move it to Wi-Fi or Ethernet, then name heaters, group them into zones and set scenes on a seven-day schedule. Affinity controls are sold separately, and the app does not work with older Bromic remote controllers.[^s1][^s2]

![Bromic app on a phone beside an Eclipse heater](img:w-eclipse-electric-w-bromic-heating-app)

## What do I need before I start?

| You need | Why |
|---|---|
| Heaters and an Affinity controller, installed by a licensed electrician | The app talks to the controller, not the heater[^s1] |
| Affinity On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller | On/Off for gas or electric; Dimmer for Platinum and Tungsten Electric; Eclipse for Eclipse Electric heat and light[^s3] |
| The controller's serial number | A 14-digit code on the control-box label[^s4] |
| Phone or tablet with Bluetooth on and internet access | Setup starts over Bluetooth[^s1] |
| A 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, or Ethernet | Remote control, schedules and voice need a network connection[^s2][^s4] |
| One email address for the site owner | The account and the Admin role sit on it[^s1] |

A manual wall switch is a complete control on its own, but it does not connect to the app.[^s2]

## How do I set up the app?

1. **Install the app** from the App Store or Google Play and create an account with the owner's email.[^s1][^s5]
2. **Create a site** and give it a plain name, such as "Deck" or "Garden".[^s1]
3. **Stand within 30 ft of the control box.** Only controllers in Bluetooth range and in discovery mode appear in the list.[^s1]
4. **Check the serial number** in the app against the label on the box, then select the controller.[^s1]
5. **Configure each channel**, one per heater circuit, and name it after the place it heats.[^s1]
6. **Connect the controller to Wi-Fi or Ethernet.** Controllers start on Bluetooth; a network connection is more stable and lets you control heat away from the patio. Ask your installer or Bromic Technical Support for network settings.[^s1]
7. **Add more controllers or users**, or finish site creation to reach the home screen.[^s1]

## What can I do from the home screen?

The home screen lists your heaters ("appliances"), zones and scenes, with an icon showing how each controller is connected: Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or Ethernet.[^s1]

- **On and off.** A toggle per heater.
- **Heat level.** Heaters on a Dimmer or Eclipse controller show a slider from 0 to 100 percent.[^s1] Eclipse heaters also dim their lighting from the same controller.[^s3]
- **Timers.** Open a heater to rename it or set a timer. Every controller also has a default four-hour timer-off countdown, adjustable to 2, 4, 6, 8 or 12 hours. Bromic advises against switching it off.[^s2]

## How do zones and scenes work?

A **zone** groups heaters you use together, such as the dining table or the lounge, so one command turns them on, sets one heat level or schedules them off.[^s1] Plan zones around how you use the space, before wiring: see [where to place patio heaters](item:RES-PP-01).

A **scene** automates a routine on a seven-day schedule: heat on at dusk on weeknights, off after an event.[^s1] Schedule-based timer-off works only on controllers connected by Wi-Fi.[^s4]

## Can other people use it?

Yes. The Admin invites people by email from Settings, Manage Users. Admins can change the site setup; Users can only operate the heaters.[^s1][^s2] Invitations need the controller on Wi-Fi.[^s1] There is no limit on heaters per site or sites per account.[^s2]

## Does it work with Alexa or Google Home?

Yes, through compatible Affinity controls; link them from the Alexa or Google Home app. Apple Home is not supported at this stage.[^s2][^s3] Voice control needs the controller on a network: it does not work over Bluetooth alone, or when the controller runs from a building management system.[^s2] For whole-home systems, see [integrating Bromic heaters with home automation](https://www.bromic.com/blog/how-to-integrate-your-bromic-smart-heat-link-with-your-home-automation-system/).

## What if the Wi-Fi drops?

The controller switches to another available connection, or to Bluetooth, and returns to Wi-Fi or Ethernet when it is back.[^s2] If the controller loses power, the app shows its heaters as offline.[^s2] [[VERIFY: heater state after a power cut and whether a wall switch can override an Affinity controller — Mike Clifford]]

## Frequently asked

### Does the app work with my older Bromic remote?
No. The app is built for Affinity controls and is not backward compatible with Bromic's legacy remote controllers.[^s1][^s2] [[VERIFY: recommended replacement path for owners of wireless On/Off and Dimmer controllers — Mike Clifford]]

### Do Affinity controllers come with a remote?
No. Affinity controls are app-based and ship without a physical remote.[^s3]

### Is the app free?
[[VERIFY: app price and any subscription — Mike Clifford]] The controllers are sold separately; see the [Bromic controls range](https://www.bromic.com/controls/).

### Where do I get help?
Bromic Technical Support at https://www.bromic.com/contact-technical-support/, or the [app page FAQ](https://www.bromic.com/affinity-smart-heat-app/).
