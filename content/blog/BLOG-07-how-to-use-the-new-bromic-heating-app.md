The Bromic heating app, listed in the stores as Bromic Smart-Heat, lets you run your Bromic heaters from your phone once compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls are installed.[^s1] You pair the controller by Bluetooth within about 30 ft, move it to Wi-Fi or Ethernet, then name your heaters, group them into zones and set scenes on a seven-day schedule. Affinity controls are sold separately, and the app does not work with older Bromic remote controllers.[^s1][^s2]

![The Bromic app on a phone beside a glowing Eclipse heater](img:w-eclipse-electric-w-bromic-heating-app)

## What You Need Before You Begin

| You need | Why |
|---|---|
| Heaters and an Affinity controller, installed by a licensed electrician | The app talks to the controller, not the heater[^s1] |
| Affinity On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller | On/Off for gas or electric; Dimmer for Platinum and Tungsten Electric; Eclipse for Eclipse Electric heat and light[^s3] |
| The controller's serial number | A 14-digit code on the control-box label[^s4] |
| Phone or tablet with Bluetooth on and internet access | Setup starts over Bluetooth[^s1] |
| A 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, or Ethernet | Remote control, schedules and voice need a network connection[^s2][^s4] |
| One email address for the site owner | The account and the Admin role sit on it[^s1] |

A manual wall switch is a complete control in its own right, though it does not connect to the app.[^s2]

## Setting Up The App

1. **Install the app** from the App Store or Google Play and create an account with the owner's email.[^s1][^s5]
2. **Create a site** and give it a simple name, such as "Deck" or "Garden".[^s1]
3. **Stand within 30 ft of the control box**, since only controllers in Bluetooth range and in discovery mode appear in the list.[^s1]
4. **Check the serial number** in the app against the label on the box, then select the controller.[^s1]
5. **Configure each channel**, one per heater circuit, and name it after the place it warms.[^s1]
6. **Connect the controller to Wi-Fi or Ethernet.** Controllers start on Bluetooth, and a network connection is more stable and lets you control the heat even when you are away from the patio. Your installer or Bromic Technical Support can help with network settings.[^s1]
7. **Add more controllers or users**, or finish site creation to reach the home screen.[^s1]

## Everything At Your Fingertips On The Home Screen

The home screen gathers your heaters ("appliances"), zones and scenes in one place, with an icon showing how each controller is connected, whether by Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or Ethernet.[^s1]

- **Switching on and off** takes a single toggle per heater.
- **Setting the heat level** is as easy as a slider from 0 to 100 percent on heaters with a Dimmer or Eclipse controller.[^s1] Eclipse heaters also dim their lighting from the same controller.[^s3]
- **Timers** sit inside each heater, where you can also rename it. Every controller also has a default four-hour timer-off countdown, adjustable to 2, 4, 6, 8 or 12 hours, and Bromic advises keeping it on.[^s2]

## Zones And Scenes For The Way You Live Outdoors

A **zone** brings together the heaters you use at the same time, such as those over the dining table or the lounge, so a single command turns them on, sets one heat level or schedules them off.[^s1] Plan your zones around how you enjoy the space before the wiring goes in; our guide on [where to place patio heaters](item:RES-PP-01) can help.

A **scene** automates a routine on a seven-day schedule, such as warmth on at dusk on weeknights and off after an event.[^s1] Schedule-based timer-off works only on controllers connected by Wi-Fi.[^s4]

## Sharing Control With Family And Staff

The Admin can invite others by email from Settings, Manage Users. Admins can change the site setup, while Users operate the heaters.[^s1][^s2] Invitations need the controller on Wi-Fi.[^s1] There is no limit on heaters per site or sites per account.[^s2]

## Voice Control With Alexa Or Google Home

Through compatible Affinity controls, you can link the app from the Alexa or Google Home app. Apple Home is not supported at this stage.[^s2][^s3] Voice control needs the controller on a network, so it does not work over Bluetooth alone, or when the controller runs from a building management system.[^s2] For whole-home systems, see [integrating Bromic heaters with home automation](https://www.bromic.com/blog/how-to-integrate-your-bromic-smart-heat-link-with-your-home-automation-system/).

## When The Wi-Fi Drops

The controller moves to another available connection, or to Bluetooth, and returns to Wi-Fi or Ethernet as soon as it is back.[^s2] If the controller loses power, the app shows its heaters as offline.[^s2] [[VERIFY: heater state after a power cut and whether a wall switch can override an Affinity controller — Product team]]

## Frequently asked

### Does the app work with my older Bromic remote?
No. The app is built for Affinity controls and is not backward compatible with Bromic's legacy remote controllers.[^s1][^s2] [[VERIFY: recommended replacement path for owners of wireless On/Off and Dimmer controllers — Product team]]

### Do Affinity controllers come with a remote?
No. Affinity controls are app-based and ship without a physical remote, so your phone becomes the control.[^s3]

### Is the app free?
[[VERIFY: app price and any subscription — Product team]] The controllers are sold separately, and you can explore the [Bromic controls range](https://www.bromic.com/controls/).

### Where do I get help?
Bromic Technical Support is at https://www.bromic.com/contact-technical-support/, and answers to common questions are in the [app page FAQ](https://www.bromic.com/affinity-smart-heat-app/).
