The outdoor living areas families love are designed as rooms, sized for what will happen there, given cover and turned toward the view and the sun. That is the approach of Yvonne Haber, an Australian residential architect who has run her own practice since 1994 and tutors at UNSW[^s1]. She includes heat as standard in her designs, because warmth is what keeps the room in use after dark and through the cooler months.

![Rear of a renovated house with open sliders and a black Tungsten Electric heater on the terrace](img:w-yvone-haber-bronte-house-1)

## Meet Yvonne Haber

Haber designs family homes, from units and terraces to semi-detached and freestanding houses. Her work centres on open plans, natural light, cross ventilation and storage. She begins with how a household lives, including habits clients may not notice in themselves, and shapes the spatial relationships around them[^s1].

Her practice is in Australia, yet the brief she describes is the one US architects now hear, with outdoor areas used as living rooms by day and late into the evening[^s1].

## Three Tests For An Outdoor Room

| Haber's test | What it means on a US project |
|---|---|
| **Proportion for the activity** | Size from the furniture out: clear space round the dining table, room for a lounge group. |
| **The option of cover** | A roof, pergola or cantilever over part of the space. It also gives heaters a mounting plane. |
| **Orientation and outlook** | Turn the room to the sun, the view and away from the prevailing wind. |

Her standard inclusions follow from these tests: a hard surface or deck for furniture, some form of outdoor heating, and soft landscaping to frame the outlook[^s1].

## What US Homeowners Are Creating Outdoors

American homeowners are building very similar rooms. In the 2026 U.S. Houzz Outdoor Trends Study, 83% of renovated outdoor spaces include a lounge or seating area[^s2], while dining areas appear in 55% and outdoor cooking in 48%[^s3].

One figure shows an architect where heating stands. Of lounge areas, 48% include a fireplace or fire pit and 66% include lighting[^s3]. A fire pit sets a lovely mood, though it warms only the people closest to it, and radiant heat over the seating keeps the whole group comfortable.

## Drawing Warmth In With The Lighting

## Frequently asked

### What makes an outdoor living area get used?
Proportions that fit the furniture, cover over at least part of the space, an orientation that suits the sun and the view, and warmth for evenings and the cooler months.

### Is a fire pit enough to heat an outdoor room?
A fire pit brings atmosphere, though it warms only the people near it. Radiant heaters mounted over the seating keep the whole group warm.

### When should heating be specified?
At design stage, together with lighting and structure, so the circuit, mounting and controls are all on the drawings.
