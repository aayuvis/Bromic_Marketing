Look for a freestanding heater that heats people, not air: radiant heat aimed at the table rather than a mushroom dome glowing in every direction. Then check the fuel you can use, how stable it is in wind, what it covers, how it is controlled, and whether it looks like it belongs. Bromic makes two: the Eclipse Smart-Heat Freestanding Electric, heat and light in one arch, and the Tungsten Smart-Heat Freestanding Gas, on propane or natural gas.

![Rooftop restaurant with arched freestanding heaters and wall-mounted heaters](img:w-eclipse-portable-hive-and-honey-1)

## Why are so many freestanding heaters clunky?

Because most are built around a fuel tank, not around the people they warm. The familiar mushroom heater puts a burner and reflector dome on a pole above a 20 lb cylinder and is typically rated at 40,000 to 48,000 BTU an hour.[^s5] It heats in a circle from above head height, and much of what it produces is warm air the breeze carries off. Pyramid and flame-tube towers add a visible flame but the same problem.

### Does wavelength matter?

Yes, especially in wind. Bromic's planning guide puts short-wave heat at about 90% radiant and instant, medium wave at about 60% with a short warm-up, and long wave at about 40%, the slowest and the most affected by wind.[^s4] On an exposed deck, choose the shorter wavelength.

## Eclipse Smart-Heat Freestanding Electric

A curved arm on a weighted, wheeled base carries a round heater over a table or lounge, with a dimmable light around its rim, so one fixture sets the warmth and the mood.[^s1] It plugs in where there is no wall or ceiling to mount on, and moves when the furniture does. Keep at least 79 in. of clearance above it.[^s2]

**Controls:** it ships with the Eclipse Dimmer Controller and remote, which step heat through 33, 66 and 100% and dim the light.[^s2] App control with zones and schedules needs a compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Eclipse controller; Wi-Fi and app features require compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately.[^s1] [[VERIFY: what app control the Eclipse Freestanding needs beyond the included dimmer — Mike Clifford]]

## Tungsten Smart-Heat Freestanding Gas

A stainless steel column with a tilting head and ceramic burners, built for open patios and restaurant terraces that need more coverage from one unit.[^s1] It runs on a 20 lb propane cylinder or on natural gas through a quick-connect line, rolls on wheels and is designed not to tip.[^s3] Bromic rates it for wind up to 8 mph.[^s3] A weatherproof [Tungsten Freestanding Cover](https://www.bromic.com/product/tungsten-freestanding-cover/) protects the head between uses.

Run time per cylinder depends on output setting and fill. [[VERIFY: run time per 20 lb cylinder; the live post said up to 8 hours — Mike Clifford]] Propane brings code limits too: portable gas heaters need set distances from overhangs and exits. *Confirm with your local fire authority and a licensed gas fitter.* For a restaurant, [what a mushroom heater really costs](https://www.bromic.com/blog/why-mushroom-heaters-are-bad-for-business/) works the fuel and cylinder maths.

## Which one should you choose?

- **No gas, a table or lounge to light and heat:** Eclipse Freestanding Electric.
- **Open patio, larger area, propane or a gas point:** Tungsten Freestanding Gas.
- **A covered patio with a soffit or wall:** a fixed heater usually suits better; see [electric outdoor heaters](https://www.bromic.com/outdoor-heaters/outdoor-electric-heaters/).

The earlier posts claimed a Bromic freestanding heater was up to three times more efficient than a mushroom heater, with savings from a 9 kg tank and $30 refills. None of those figures had a method or source, so they are not repeated here.[^s6]

## Frequently asked

### Is a freestanding patio heater the same as a portable one?

Bromic now calls them freestanding; some older pages say portable. Both mean a heater on its own base that can be moved, not fixed to a wall or ceiling.

### How many freestanding heaters do I need?

Divide the area you want warm by each heater's coverage, about 121 ft² for Eclipse and 215 ft² for Tungsten,[^s1] then check the layout. The [sizing calculator](asset:A03) or the [design service](https://www.bromic.com/design-your-space/) does it for you.

### Can I use a freestanding heater under a cover?

An electric one, within its clearances. Gas freestanding heaters need open air and set distances from overhangs; check the manual and your local code.
