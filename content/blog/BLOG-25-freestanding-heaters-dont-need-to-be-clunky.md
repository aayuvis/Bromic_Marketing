The right freestanding heater warms the people around your table, sending radiant heat exactly where they sit instead of glowing in every direction from a mushroom dome. From there, consider the fuel you can use, how steady it stands in the wind, the area it covers, how you control it and whether it looks as though it belongs. Bromic makes two: the Eclipse Smart-Heat Freestanding Electric, with heat and light in one graceful arch, and the Tungsten Smart-Heat Freestanding Gas, on propane or natural gas.

## Why So Many Freestanding Heaters Feel Clunky

Most freestanding heaters are built around a fuel tank first, and around the people they warm second. The familiar mushroom heater sets a burner and reflector dome on a pole above a 20 lb cylinder and is typically rated at 40,000 to 48,000 BTU an hour.[^s5] It heats in a circle from above head height, and much of what it produces is warm air that the breeze soon carries off. Pyramid and flame-tube towers add a visible flame, yet they lose their warmth to the breeze in much the same way.

### Does wavelength matter?

It does, especially when the wind picks up. Our planning guide puts short-wave heat at about 90% radiant and instant, medium wave at about 60% with a short warm-up, and long wave at about 40%, the slowest to warm and the most affected by wind.[^s4] On an exposed deck, the shorter wavelength will keep your guests comfortable.

## Frequently asked

### Is a freestanding patio heater the same as a portable one?

Yes. Bromic now calls them freestanding, while some older pages say portable. Both describe a heater on its own base that can be moved, rather than one fixed to a wall or ceiling.

### How many freestanding heaters do I need?

Divide the area you want to keep warm by each heater's coverage, about 121 ft² for Eclipse and 215 ft² for Tungsten,[^s1] then check the layout. The [sizing calculator](asset:A03) or our [design service](https://www.bromic.com/design-your-space/) can do it for you.

### Can I use a freestanding heater under a cover?

An electric one can be used under a cover, within its clearances. Gas freestanding heaters need open air and set distances from overhangs, so check the manual and your local code.
