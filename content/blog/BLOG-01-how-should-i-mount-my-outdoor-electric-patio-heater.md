Mount a Bromic electric heater in one of three ways: from the ceiling on a bracket or pole, flush in the ceiling with a recess kit, or on a wall bracket tilted toward the seating. Platinum and Tungsten Electric sit at least 72 in above the floor on a wall and 96 in on a ceiling[^s3][^s4]. Every heater runs on a dedicated circuit with Affinity Smart-Heat controls and a wall switch, installed by a licensed electrician.

*Follow the installation manual for the exact model. Electrical work must be done by a licensed electrician to the code adopted locally; confirm requirements with the local building authority.*

## Which mounting method suits my space?

Start with what is overhead. A solid ceiling or beam at the right height points to ceiling mounting; a ceiling cavity points to recessing; no ceiling, or a vaulted or angled one, points to the wall[^s1][^s2].

| Method | Suits | Heaters | Key figure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ceiling (bracket or pole) | Covered patios, pergolas, high ceilings | Platinum, Tungsten, Cobalt, Eclipse Electric | Heater at least 96 in above the floor (Cobalt 8 ft)[^s3][^s5] |
| Recessed (flush) | New builds and retrofits with a ceiling cavity | Platinum, Platinum Marine, Tungsten | Cavity from 7.1 in to 21.1 in by kit[^s1] |
| Wall bracket | Open patios, vaulted or angled ceilings | Platinum, Platinum Marine, Tungsten, Cobalt | At least 72 in above the floor (Cobalt 6 ft); tilt 15–45 degrees by model[^s1][^s3] |

## How high, how heavy and how much current?

Design the fixing and the circuit for the model in the box. The older figure of 16–22 lb for every electric heater is out of date: the 4500 W Platinum weighs 33 lb and Eclipse Electric 30 lb[^s3][^s5].

| Model | Wall min. | Ceiling min. | Weight | Amps at 240 V | Amps at 208 V |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Platinum 2300 W | 72 in | 96 in | 17 lb | 9.6 A | 11.1 A |
| Platinum 3400 W | 72 in | 96 in | 22 lb | 14.2 A | 16.3 A |
| Platinum 4500 W | 72 in | 96 in | 33 lb | 18.8 A | 21.6 A |
| Tungsten 2000–6000 W | 72 in | 96 in | [[VERIFY: per model — Mike Clifford]] | [[VERIFY: per model — Mike Clifford]] | [[VERIFY: per model — Mike Clifford]] |
| Cobalt 4000 W | 6 ft | 8 ft | 19.8 lb | 16.6 A | 18.75 A |
| Cobalt 6000 W | 6 ft | 8 ft | 22 lb | 25 A | 28.4 A |
| Eclipse Electric 2900 W | Pole mount | 96 in | 30 lb | 12.1 A | 13.9 A |

Sources: product pages[^s3][^s4][^s5]. Platinum Marine shares the Platinum weights and heights. Tungsten 4000 and 6000 W are also built for 277 V and have two separately controllable elements[^s4]. Order a 208 V or 240 V heater to match the supply; a heater on the wrong voltage will not give its rated output.

## Should I ceiling mount my heater?

Ceiling mounting suits covered spaces and is available across the electric range[^s1]. The heater must hang perpendicular to the floor, so an angled ceiling needs a wedge[^s1]. On high ceilings, a pole brings the heater down: an 8 in pole comes with every Platinum heater, with 2 ft and 4 ft poles at [Platinum mounting poles](https://www.bromic.com/product/platinum-electric-mounting-poles/) and a 3 ft pole for [Tungsten](https://www.bromic.com/product/tungsten-electric-mounting-poles/)[^s3]. Pole choices per collection are in [our ceiling-mounting guide](https://www.bromic.com/blog/top-5-considerations-for-installing-ceiling-mounted-outdoor-heaters/). See the range at [ceiling-mounted heaters](https://www.bromic.com/outdoor-heaters/ceiling-mounted/).

## What should I consider before recessing?

Recessing gives a flush ceiling and is available for Platinum, Platinum Marine and Tungsten Electric[^s1]. The cavity decides the kit:

- Tungsten Electric recess kit: at least 21.1 in [[VERIFY: 21.1 in here vs 20 in in the recessing post — Mike Clifford]][^s1]
- Platinum standard recess kit: at least 10 in, varying by model[^s1]
- Platinum Low Clearance Recess Kit: as little as 7.1 in, for new builds and retrofits[^s1]

Keep clear of lights and fans in the ceiling[^s1]. The full checklist is in [what to consider when recessing electric heaters](https://www.bromic.com/blog/what-should-be-considered-when-installing-recessed-electric-outdoor-heaters/); see also [flush-mounted heaters](https://www.bromic.com/outdoor-heaters/flush-mounted/).

## Is wall mounting the right choice?

Wall mounting suits patios with no ceiling, or a vaulted or angled one, and keeps the floor clear[^s1][^s2]. Check four things:

1. **Height.** At least 72 in to the heater on Platinum and Tungsten, 6 ft on Cobalt[^s3][^s5].
2. **Structure.** The wall or beam must carry the heater's weight from the table above, fixed to blocking[^s2].
3. **Aim.** Tilt the heater 15–45 degrees, by model, at the seating or dining area so heat is not wasted[^s1][^s2].
4. **Combustibles.** Keep at least 36 in from combustibles: anything not rated for about 149°F above ambient, such as canvas, awnings, screens, vinyl, TVs, lights, fans, sprinklers and outlets[^s2]. Clearance above the heater varies by model and wattage [[VERIFY: electric top clearance — the live posts give 5 in, 6–12 in, 8–13 in and 5 ft; publish one figure per model from the manual — Mike Clifford]].

Brackets: [Platinum](https://www.bromic.com/product/platinum-electric-wall-mounting-bracket/) and [Tungsten](https://www.bromic.com/product/tungsten-electric-wall-mounting-bracket/). See the range at [wall-mounted heaters](https://www.bromic.com/outdoor-heaters/wall-mounted/).

## Which controls do I wire with each heater?

Controls are part of the install, not an extra. Each heater needs an Affinity Smart-Heat controller and a manual wall switch[^s3][^s7].

| Control | What it runs | Wiring notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Affinity On/Off](https://www.bromic.com/product/affinity-smart-heat-on-off-controllers/) | Platinum, Tungsten, Cobalt | 1, 2 or 5 channels; up to 6 kW per channel at 208–277 V[^s6] |
| [Affinity Dimmer](https://www.bromic.com/product/affinity-smart-heat-dimmer-controllers/) | Platinum, Tungsten | Same channels and load; dims in 1% steps[^s6] |
| [Affinity Eclipse controller](https://www.bromic.com/product/affinity-smart-heat-eclipse-controllers/) | Eclipse Electric | One heater per channel[^s6] |
| [Manual wall switch](https://www.bromic.com/product/manual-wall-switch/) | All of the above | 20 A, 120–277 V; indoor or outdoor kit[^s6] |
| [Expansion board](https://www.bromic.com/product/affinity-smart-heat-expansion-board/) | BMS or home automation | Ethernet and 0–10 V; BMS mode disables the app[^s6] |

Plan the circuit and the controller together:

- Give each heater, or each controller channel, a dedicated circuit sized for continuous load. The maths is in [what size breaker a 4500 W heater needs](item:BLD-IN-KB1).
- Check GFCI requirements with the inspector before rough-in: [does an outdoor electric heater need GFCI?](item:BLD-BY-KB1)
- Place the controller where it reaches 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, and within about 30 ft for Bluetooth setup[^s6].
- The Bromic Smart-Heat app adds zones, schedules and timers. Wi-Fi and app features require compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately[^s7].

## Which codes apply?

In the US, electric heaters are installed to ANSI/NFPA 70, the [National Electrical Code](https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/nfpa-70-standard-development/70)[^s8][^s9]. Cities and states adopt editions on their own timetable, and the installation manual may set stricter limits. Confirm requirements with the local building authority and have a licensed electrician do the work.

## Frequently asked

### How high should a wall-mounted electric patio heater be?

At least 72 in from the floor to the heater for Platinum and Tungsten Electric, and 6 ft for Cobalt[^s3][^s5]. The installation manual for the exact model governs.

### How high should a ceiling-mounted electric heater be?

At least 96 in above the floor for Platinum, Tungsten and Eclipse Electric, and 8 ft for Cobalt[^s3][^s5]. On higher ceilings, use a mounting pole.

### Can I mount an electric heater on an angled ceiling?

Yes, with a wedge so the heater hangs perpendicular to the floor, or mount it on a wall instead[^s1].

### Do I need a controller for every heater?

Every heater needs an Affinity Smart-Heat controller and a manual wall switch. One controller channel can run several heaters up to its rated load[^s6].
