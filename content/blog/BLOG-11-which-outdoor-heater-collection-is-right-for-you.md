The right Bromic heater depends on three things: the space (open, covered or semi-enclosed), the fuel you have (electric or gas) and how you want to mount it (recessed, ceiling, wall or freestanding). Electric suits covered and semi-enclosed patios; gas suits large, well-ventilated spaces; freestanding suits open ground with no structure. Every installed heater needs its controls: an Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller, or a manual wall switch.[^s1]

## Do patio heaters actually work outdoors?

Radiant (infrared) heaters do, because they warm people and surfaces rather than the air, so a light breeze does not carry the heat away.[^s8] Fan heaters and convection heaters warm air, which outdoors simply drifts off. Bromic makes only radiant heaters, electric and gas. How well they work comes down to output, mounting height and placement, which is why sizing matters more than brand.

## The electric families

**Tungsten Electric.** The workhorse: 2000, 3000, 4000 and 6000 W, covering about 65, 100, 130 and 160 ft², in black (and white at 4000 and 6000 W). Wall, ceiling or recessed. The two larger sizes have two separately controllable elements. Warranty: 2 years on the heater, 7 on the elements.[^s2]

**Platinum Electric.** Slim, low-glare and designed to disappear into a ceiling: 2300, 3400 and 4500 W, covering about 65, 108 and 143 ft², in black or white, with recess and low-clearance recess kits. Warranty: 3 years.[^s3] The **Marine** version is AISI 316 stainless steel, rated IPX5 and approved by Lloyd's Register for marine use; warranty 5 years on land, 3 on vessels.[^s4]

**Cobalt Electric.** A value option at 4000 and 6000 W, about 130 and 160 ft².[^s5]

**Eclipse Electric.** Heat and dimmable rim lighting in one disc: 2900 W, about 121 ft², stepping between 33, 66 and 100 percent output.[^s1][^s6] Mounted on straight, curved, twin or wall poles.

**Eclipse Freestanding.** The same disc on a curved arm with a weighted base and wheels. It plugs in, so there is nothing to install beyond a suitable outlet.[^s1]

**Tungsten Coastal and Select (coming soon).** New Tungsten collections, about 64 to 144 ft²: Coastal in 316 marine-grade stainless steel, Select with extra finishes.[^s1]

Controls for electric: Platinum, Tungsten and Cobalt run on an Affinity On/Off (full heat) or Dimmer (heat in 1% steps) controller, or a manual wall switch.[^s9] Eclipse Electric runs on the Affinity Eclipse controller, which sets heat and light together.[^s6] [[VERIFY: whether Eclipse Electric still ships with the Eclipse Dimmer Controller and remote as an alternative to the Affinity Eclipse controller — Mike Clifford]]

## The gas families

**Platinum Gas.** Two sizes, 23,600 and 39,800 BTU, covering about 160 and 215 ft², on natural gas or propane, wall or ceiling mounted.[^s7]

**Tungsten Gas.** Two sizes on a pivot arm for wall or ceiling. [[VERIFY: Tungsten Gas output and coverage per size, unpublished in content until confirmed — Mike Clifford]]

**Tungsten Freestanding Gas.** About 38,000 BTU and 215 ft², on propane or natural gas, with a tilting head, wheels and an anti-tilt design.[^s1] [[VERIFY: 38,000 BTU (Lookbook) vs 38,500 BTU (product page) — Mike Clifford]]

Gas heaters need open air around them. Bromic treats a space as outdoor when at least 30% of its perimeter is permanently open.[^s8] [[VERIFY: the merged post said 25%; confirm the current ventilation rule — Mike Clifford]] Gas heaters switch on and off; they do not dim. Run them from an Affinity On/Off controller, up to 10 heaters per channel, or a manual wall switch.[^s7] A licensed gas fitter installs fixed gas heaters; confirm clearances and ventilation with your local authority.

## How many heaters do I need?

Size by each heater's coverage, not by a rule of thumb. A 12 × 20 ft covered dining area (240 ft²) might take two 4000 W Tungsten Electric heaters, or a mix of zones at different outputs. Mounting height and wind change the answer, so use the [sizing calculator](item:A03) or Bromic's free [Design Your Space](https://www.bromic.com/design-your-space/) service, which lays out heaters and controls from your photos or plans. ## Frequently asked

### What is the best type of outdoor heater?
Radiant (infrared). It warms people directly and copes with light wind.[^s8] Choose electric or gas by what the site has and how open it is.

### Can I mix collections?
Yes. Many projects pair recessed electric heaters over dining with an Eclipse over the lounge, each on its own zone and controller.

### Do I need controls?
Yes. Every fixed heater needs an Affinity controller or a manual wall switch. App, schedules and voice need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls, sold separately.[^s1] See [the Bromic controls range](https://www.bromic.com/controls/).
