The right Bromic heater begins with three questions about your outdoor space: how open it is (open, covered or semi-enclosed), which fuel you have (electric or gas) and how you would like the heat to sit (recessed, ceiling, wall or freestanding). Electric is at home on covered and semi-enclosed patios, gas in large, well-ventilated spaces, and freestanding on open ground with no structure overhead. Every installed heater is paired with its controls – an Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller, or a manual wall switch.[^s1]

## Do Patio Heaters Really Work Outdoors?

Radiant (infrared) heaters do, because they warm the people and surfaces around them instead of the air, so a light breeze cannot carry the comfort away.[^s8] Fan and convection heaters warm the air, which outdoors simply drifts off into the evening. Bromic makes only radiant heaters, in electric and gas. How well any heater performs comes down to its output, mounting height and placement, which is why careful sizing matters more than the name on the badge.

## The Electric Collections

**Tungsten Electric** is the dependable all-rounder of the range, offered at 2000, 3000, 4000 and 6000 W to cover about 65, 100, 130 and 160 ft², in black (and in white at 4000 and 6000 W). It mounts on a wall or ceiling or recesses neatly into one, and the two larger sizes carry two separately controllable elements. It is backed by a 2-year warranty on the heater and 7 years on the elements.[^s2]

**Platinum Electric** is slim, low-glare and designed to disappear into a ceiling, offered at 2300, 3400 and 4500 W to cover about 65, 108 and 143 ft², in black or white, with recess and low-clearance recess kits and a 3-year warranty.[^s3] The **Marine** version is crafted in AISI 316 stainless steel, rated IPX5 and approved by Lloyd's Register for marine use, with a warranty of 5 years on land and 3 on vessels.[^s4]

**Cobalt Electric** brings value to larger spaces at 4000 and 6000 W, covering about 130 and 160 ft².[^s5]

**Eclipse Electric** brings heat and dimmable rim lighting together in one refined disc – 2900 W across about 121 ft², stepping gently between 33, 66 and 100 percent output.[^s1][^s6] It mounts on straight, curved, twin or wall poles.

**Eclipse Freestanding** sets the same disc on a curved arm with a weighted base and wheels. It plugs in, so all it asks of your space is a suitable outlet.[^s1]

**Tungsten Coastal and Select (coming soon)** are new Tungsten collections covering about 64 to 144 ft², with Coastal crafted in 316 marine-grade stainless steel and Select offering extra finishes.[^s1]

For the electric collections, Platinum, Tungsten and Cobalt run on an Affinity On/Off (full heat) or Dimmer (heat in 1% steps) controller, or a manual wall switch.[^s9] Eclipse Electric runs on the Affinity Eclipse controller, which sets heat and light together.[^s6] [[VERIFY: whether Eclipse Electric still ships with the Eclipse Dimmer Controller and remote as an alternative to the Affinity Eclipse controller — Product team]]

## The Gas Collections

**Platinum Gas** comes in two sizes, 23,600 and 39,800 BTU, covering about 160 and 215 ft² on natural gas or propane, mounted on a wall or ceiling.[^s7]

**Tungsten Gas** offers two sizes on a pivot arm for wall or ceiling mounting. [[VERIFY: Tungsten Gas output and coverage per size, unpublished in content until confirmed — Product team]]

**Tungsten Freestanding Gas** delivers about 38,000 BTU across 215 ft² on propane or natural gas, with a tilting head, wheels and an anti-tilt design.[^s1] [[VERIFY: 38,000 BTU (Lookbook) vs 38,500 BTU (product page) — Product team]]

Gas heaters need open air around them, and Bromic treats a space as outdoor when at least 30% of its perimeter is permanently open.[^s8] [[VERIFY: the merged post said 25%; confirm the current ventilation rule — Product team]] Gas heaters switch on and off and do not dim, so they run from an Affinity On/Off controller, with up to 10 heaters per channel, or a manual wall switch.[^s7] A licensed gas fitter installs fixed gas heaters; confirm clearances and ventilation with your local authority.

## How Many Heaters Will Your Space Need?

Each heater's coverage is the surest guide, far more reliable than a rule of thumb. A 12 × 20 ft covered dining area (240 ft²) might take two 4000 W Tungsten Electric heaters, or a mix of zones at different outputs. Mounting height and wind both shape the answer, so try the [sizing calculator](item:A03) or Bromic's free [Design Your Space](https://www.bromic.com/design-your-space/) service, where we lay out your heaters and controls from your photos or plans. ## Frequently Asked

### What is the best type of outdoor heater?
Radiant (infrared) heat, which warms people directly and holds up well in a light wind.[^s8] Choose electric or gas by what your site offers and how open it is.

### Can I mix collections?
Yes. Many projects pair recessed electric heaters over the dining table with an Eclipse over the lounge, each on its own zone and controller.

### Do I need controls?
Yes. Every fixed heater needs an Affinity controller or a manual wall switch, and the app, schedules and voice control need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat controls, sold separately.[^s1] See [the Bromic controls range](https://www.bromic.com/controls/).
