A Bromic gas heater can hang from the ceiling on its bracket or a drop pole, or sit on the wall bracket supplied with it. In either position the heater sits at least 96 in above the floor, with at least 36 in clear above it (14 in with a heat deflector) and 36 in from combustibles[^s2][^s3]. Each heater also needs a 110 V supply, an Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off controller and a wall switch.

*Gas work must be done by a licensed gas fitter and electrical work by a licensed electrician, to the installation manual and the codes adopted locally. Confirm requirements with the local building authority.*

## Choosing Between The Ceiling And The Wall

| | Ceiling mount | Wall mount |
|---|---|---|
| Suits | Covered and semi-enclosed spaces with a ceiling or beam that can carry the heater | Open-air patios; ceilings that are missing, angled, weak or obstructed[^s1] |
| Hardware | Bracket, or one of six [gas mounting poles](https://www.bromic.com/product/gas-mounting-poles/)[^s5] | [Gas wall mounting bracket](https://www.bromic.com/product/gas-wall-mounting-bracket/), supplied with each heater[^s1] |
| Height | At least 96 in; 8–9 ft recommended[^s3][^s5] | At least 96 in[^s2] |
| Heaters | [Platinum Gas](https://www.bromic.com/product/platinum-smart-heat-gas/), [Tungsten Gas](https://www.bromic.com/product/tungsten-smart-heat-gas/) | Platinum Gas, Tungsten Gas |

Our earlier post said ceiling mounting works with "ceilings of any height". More precisely, it works with any ceiling high enough to place the heater at 96 in or more. Where a low ceiling cannot give that height along with the top clearance, a different heater or location will serve your space better.

## Finding The Right Drop Pole

Gas poles come in six lengths: 9.69, 13.62, 17.56, 25.43, 33.31 and 41.18 in[^s5] [[VERIFY: pole lengths and the 16.6 in heater-and-bracket height against the 2026 cut sheet — Product team]]. To choose yours, take the ceiling height and subtract both the target heater height (8–9 ft) and the 16.6 in heater and bracket[^s5]; the nearest shorter pole is your length. The pole must hang straight down, and its adjustment point is intended for a pitched ceiling only[^s5].

## The Clearances That Keep Your Space Safe

| Clearance | Without heat deflector | With heat deflector |
|---|---|---|
| Floor to heater | At least 96 in | At least 96 in |
| Above the heater (ceiling, soffit, beam) | At least 36 in | At least 14 in |
| To combustibles | At least 36 in | At least 36 in |

Sources: wall-mounting post and deflector pages[^s1][^s2][^s5]. Combustibles include fabrics, plastics and powder-coated finishes[^s1]. The [Platinum Gas](https://www.bromic.com/product/platinum-gas-heat-deflector/) and [Tungsten Gas](https://www.bromic.com/product/tungsten-gas-heat-deflector/) deflectors are crafted from dual-layer stainless steel[^s5]. Bromic recommends a deflector wherever space above is tight or the heater is exposed, and the product pages set that threshold at 1,100 mm (about 43 in) for Platinum Gas and 42 in for Tungsten Gas[^s3][^s4] [[VERIFY: one deflector threshold, and whether 36 in is the minimum without one — Product team]]. The 14 in given here and the 35 cm quoted in our covered-patio post are the same clearance expressed in two units.

## The Controls And Wiring Behind Every Gas Heater

Gas heaters still rely on electricity, and each takes a 110 V, 2 A supply for ignition and control[^s3][^s4]. Run that circuit alongside the gas line.

- **The Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off controller** offers 1, 2 or 5 channels, with up to 10 gas heaters per channel[^s6]. Gas heaters are on/off only and do not dim[^s3].
- **The manual wall switch** is rated 20 A, 120–277 V, with an indoor or outdoor kit[^s6].
- **The expansion board** connects the system to a building management or home automation system[^s3].
- **The Bromic Smart-Heat app** adds zones, schedules and timers. Wi-Fi and app features require compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately[^s7].

## The Codes Behind A Safe Installation

In the US, gas heaters are installed to ANSI Z223.1/[NFPA 54](https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/nfpa-54-standard-development/54), and the electrical supply to [NFPA 70](https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/nfpa-70-standard-development/70)[^s8][^s9][^s10]. Choose heaters carrying an Intertek, UL or CSA mark[^s8]. A licensed gas fitter sizes the gas line and fits the shut-off, and permits should be confirmed with the local building authority.

## Frequently asked

### How high should a gas patio heater be mounted?

At least 96 in from the floor to the heater, on a wall or ceiling. Bromic recommends 8–9 ft for the most comfortable warmth at seating height[^s3][^s5].

### What does a heat deflector do?

It shields the surface above the heater and reduces the top clearance from 36 in to 14 in[^s2][^s5].

### Can I wall mount a gas heater on a covered patio?

Yes, as long as the ceiling above is at least 36 in clear of the heater, or 14 in with a deflector, and the heater is 36 in from combustibles[^s2].

### Do gas heaters need electricity?

Yes. Each needs a 110 V, 2 A supply, an Affinity On/Off controller and a wall switch[^s3][^s6].
