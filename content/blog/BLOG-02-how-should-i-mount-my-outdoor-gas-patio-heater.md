Mount a Bromic gas heater from the ceiling, on its bracket or a drop pole, or on the wall bracket supplied with it. Either way the heater sits at least 96 in above the floor, with at least 36 in clear above it (14 in with a heat deflector) and 36 in from combustibles[^s2][^s3]. Each heater also needs a 110 V supply, an Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off controller and a wall switch.

*Gas work must be done by a licensed gas fitter and electrical work by a licensed electrician, to the installation manual and the codes adopted locally. Confirm requirements with the local building authority.*

## Ceiling or wall: which suits my space?

| | Ceiling mount | Wall mount |
|---|---|---|
| Suits | Covered and semi-enclosed spaces with a ceiling or beam that can carry the heater | Open-air patios; ceilings that are missing, angled, weak or obstructed[^s1] |
| Hardware | Bracket, or one of six [gas mounting poles](https://www.bromic.com/product/gas-mounting-poles/)[^s5] | [Gas wall mounting bracket](https://www.bromic.com/product/gas-wall-mounting-bracket/), supplied with each heater[^s1] |
| Height | At least 96 in; 8–9 ft recommended[^s3][^s5] | At least 96 in[^s2] |
| Heaters | [Platinum Gas](https://www.bromic.com/product/platinum-smart-heat-gas/), [Tungsten Gas](https://www.bromic.com/product/tungsten-smart-heat-gas/) | Platinum Gas, Tungsten Gas |

The older post said ceiling mounting works with "ceilings of any height". It works with any ceiling high enough to put the heater at 96 in or more; a low ceiling that cannot give that height and the top clearance needs a different heater or location.

## How do I choose a drop pole?

Gas poles come in six lengths: 9.69, 13.62, 17.56, 25.43, 33.31 and 41.18 in[^s5] [[VERIFY: pole lengths and the 16.6 in heater-and-bracket height against the 2026 cut sheet — Mike Clifford]]. To pick one, take the ceiling height, subtract the target heater height (8–9 ft) and the 16.6 in heater and bracket[^s5]; the nearest shorter pole is your length. The pole must hang straight down; its adjustment point is for a pitched ceiling only[^s5].

## What clearances does a gas heater need?

| Clearance | Without heat deflector | With heat deflector |
|---|---|---|
| Floor to heater | At least 96 in | At least 96 in |
| Above the heater (ceiling, soffit, beam) | At least 36 in | At least 14 in |
| To combustibles | At least 36 in | At least 36 in |

Sources: wall-mounting post and deflector pages[^s1][^s2][^s5]. Combustibles include fabrics, plastics and powder-coated finishes[^s1]. The [Platinum Gas](https://www.bromic.com/product/platinum-gas-heat-deflector/) and [Tungsten Gas](https://www.bromic.com/product/tungsten-gas-heat-deflector/) deflectors are dual-layer stainless steel[^s5]. Bromic recommends one where space above is tight or the heater is exposed: the product pages set that threshold at 1,100 mm (about 43 in) for Platinum Gas and 42 in for Tungsten Gas[^s3][^s4] [[VERIFY: one deflector threshold, and whether 36 in is the minimum without one — Mike Clifford]]. Fourteen inches and the 35 cm quoted in the covered-patio post are the same clearance in two units.

## Which controls and wiring does a gas heater need?

Gas heaters still need electricity: each takes a 110 V, 2 A supply for ignition and control[^s3][^s4]. Run that circuit with the gas line.

- **Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off controller**: 1, 2 or 5 channels, up to 10 gas heaters per channel[^s6]. Gas heaters are on/off only; they do not dim[^s3].
- **Manual wall switch**: 20 A, 120–277 V, indoor or outdoor kit[^s6].
- **Expansion board**: for a building management or home automation system[^s3].
- **Bromic Smart-Heat app**: zones, schedules and timers. Wi-Fi and app features require compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately[^s7].

## Which codes apply?

In the US, gas heaters are installed to ANSI Z223.1/[NFPA 54](https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/nfpa-54-standard-development/54), and the electrical supply to [NFPA 70](https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/nfpa-70-standard-development/70)[^s8][^s9][^s10]. Use heaters carrying an Intertek, UL or CSA mark[^s8]. A licensed gas fitter sizes the gas line and fits the shut-off; confirm permits with the local building authority.

## Frequently asked

### How high should a gas patio heater be mounted?

At least 96 in from the floor to the heater, on a wall or ceiling. Bromic recommends 8–9 ft for the best heat at seating height[^s3][^s5].

### What does a heat deflector do?

It shields the surface above the heater, reducing the top clearance from 36 in to 14 in[^s2][^s5].

### Can I wall mount a gas heater on a covered patio?

Yes, if the ceiling above is at least 36 in clear of the heater, or 14 in with a deflector, and the heater is 36 in from combustibles[^s2].

### Do gas heaters need electricity?

Yes. Each needs a 110 V, 2 A supply, an Affinity On/Off controller and a wall switch[^s3][^s6].
