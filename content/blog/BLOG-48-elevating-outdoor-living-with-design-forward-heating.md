The most beautiful outdoor rooms choose their heating alongside the lighting and the ceiling, with a mounting that suits the architecture, whether recessed into a soffit, flush to a ceiling, on a wall or hung from a pole as a feature. Built-in heaters keep the floor clear and the lines clean, while a freestanding heater added later can look like an afterthought. The earlier you decide, the more gracefully the heater disappears. Only 23% of homeowners plan heating at the start[^s7].

![Covered deck with a cedar ceiling and a heater mounted above lounge seats beside a forest](img:w-print-15)

## When Heating Arrives Too Late

Too often, heating is the final decision. The patio is finished, the first cool evening arrives, and a tall portable heater ends up in the corner. Designer Danny Wang put it simply: a heater should follow the design language of the space rather than interrupt it[^s2]. That only happens when someone chooses it together with the materials and the ceiling.

## Finding The Mounting That Suits Your Look

| Look you want | Mounting | Heater that fits |
|---|---|---|
| Invisible: heat without a visible fixture | Recessed flush into the soffit or pergola | Platinum Smart-Heat Electric, with a recess kit[^s4] |
| Clean and minimal | Ceiling or wall, close to the surface | Platinum Smart-Heat Electric, black or white[^s4] |
| Industrial or graphic | Wall or ceiling, exposed | Tungsten Smart-Heat Electric[^s5] |
| A feature: heat and light as one object | Hung from a single, double or curved pole | Eclipse Smart-Heat Electric[^s4] |

Platinum Smart-Heat Electric comes in 2300, 3400 and 4500 W and covers approx. 65–143 ft² depending on model and installation[^s4]. Tungsten Smart-Heat Electric runs from 2,000 to 6,000 W and covers approx. 65–160 ft²[^s5]. Eclipse Smart-Heat Electric covers approx. 121 ft² and adds dimmable rim lighting[^s4].

## Fire Pits And Heaters, Each With Its Own Role

A fire pit is a focal point that brings atmosphere, though it is not a dependable way to keep a whole group warm through the season[^s2]. Radiant heaters warm people and surfaces directly and hold up in a light wind[^s6]. Design for both, with the fire pit setting the mood and the heater over the seating bringing the comfort.

## Warmth And Light, Woven Into The Evening

Heat and light together shape the feel of an evening outdoors. Plan the heater positions with the lighting plan, and where you want the fixture itself to set the mood, choose a heater that offers both. Eclipse combines heat with dimmable light from a single fitting[^s4]. Elsewhere, keep the heaters recessed and let the lights carry the look.

![Arched heater over lounge seating on a desert terrace](img:w-bromiceclipseheater-scottsdalehome-4)

## Controls That Belong To The Design

The controls deserve the same care as the heaters, so there is never a stray remote on a side table or a switch in an awkward place. Specify an Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off or Dimmer controller (or the Eclipse controller for Eclipse), a wall switch placed with the light switches, and the Bromic Smart-Heat app for zones and schedules. Wi-Fi and app features require compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately. Affinity also connects with Control4, Crestron and Lutron, though compatibility varies by system[^s4].

## Frequently asked

### What is the least visible outdoor heater?
A heater recessed flush into the ceiling or pergola. Platinum Smart-Heat Electric can be recessed with a recess kit[^s4].

### Can an outdoor heater also be a light?
Yes. Eclipse Smart-Heat Electric combines radiant heat with dimmable light in one fitting[^s4].

### When should I choose the heater?
Choose it with the ceiling, lighting and electrical plan, so the recess, circuit and switch go in once. A licensed electrician should confirm the circuit and mounting.
