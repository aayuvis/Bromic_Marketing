Choose heating at the same time as the lighting and the ceiling, and pick a mounting that suits the architecture: recessed into a soffit, flush to a ceiling, on a wall, or hung from a pole as a feature. Built-in heaters keep the floor clear and the lines clean, where a freestanding heater added later can look like an afterthought. The fewer decisions left to the end, the less the heater shows. Only 23% of homeowners plan heating at the start[^s7].

![Covered deck with a cedar ceiling and a heater mounted above lounge seats beside a forest](img:w-print-15)

## Why does heating so often spoil the design?

Because it arrives last. The patio is finished, the first cool evening comes, and a tall portable heater goes in the corner. Designer Danny Wang put it simply: a heater should follow the design language of the space, not interrupt it[^s2]. That is only possible if someone chose it with the materials and the ceiling.

## Which mounting suits which look?

| Look you want | Mounting | Heater that fits |
|---|---|---|
| Invisible: heat without a visible fixture | Recessed flush into the soffit or pergola | Platinum Smart-Heat Electric, with a recess kit[^s4] |
| Clean and minimal | Ceiling or wall, close to the surface | Platinum Smart-Heat Electric, black or white[^s4] |
| Industrial or graphic | Wall or ceiling, exposed | Tungsten Smart-Heat Electric[^s5] |
| A feature: heat and light as one object | Hung from a single, double or curved pole | Eclipse Smart-Heat Electric[^s4] |

Platinum Smart-Heat Electric comes in 2300, 3400 and 4500 W and covers approx. 65–143 ft² depending on model and installation[^s4]. Tungsten Smart-Heat Electric runs from 2,000 to 6,000 W and covers approx. 65–160 ft²[^s5]. Eclipse Smart-Heat Electric covers approx. 121 ft² and adds dimmable rim lighting[^s4].

## Do fire pits and heaters do the same job?

No. A fire pit is a focal point and gives atmosphere; it is not a dependable way to keep a whole group warm through the season[^s2]. Radiant heaters warm people and surfaces directly and hold up in light wind[^s6]. Design both: the fire pit for the mood, the heater over the seating for the comfort.

## How do heat and light work together?

They are one layer of the evening. Plan the heater positions with the lighting plan, and where you want the fixture itself to set the mood, choose a heater that gives both. Eclipse combines heat with dimmable light from one fitting[^s4]. Elsewhere, keep heaters recessed and let the lights carry the look.

## What controls does a design-led install need?

The controls are part of the design: no remote left on a side table, no switch in an odd place. Specify an Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off or Dimmer controller (or the Eclipse controller for Eclipse), a wall switch placed with the light switches, and the Bromic Smart-Heat app for zones and schedules. Wi-Fi and app features require compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately. Affinity also connects with Control4, Crestron and Lutron, though compatibility varies by system[^s4].

## Frequently asked

### What is the least visible outdoor heater?
A heater recessed flush into the ceiling or pergola. Platinum Smart-Heat Electric can be recessed with a recess kit[^s4].

### Can an outdoor heater also be a light?
Yes. Eclipse Smart-Heat Electric combines radiant heat with dimmable light in one fitting[^s4].

### When should I choose the heater?
With the ceiling, lighting and electrical plan, so the recess, circuit and switch go in once. A licensed electrician should confirm the circuit and mounting.
