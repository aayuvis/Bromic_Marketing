# Outdoor Living Trends 2026: corrections and stat verification

The report reads well and its argument holds: plan heating early. What lets it down is the detail. Five typos, one broken sentence, an insight on the wrong page, and citations that a buyer, an architect or an AI search engine cannot trace. Two statistics do not survive a source check and come out. Most of the rest need a wording fix to say exactly what the survey measured.

This file has three parts: the corrections table for the designer, the statistic check, and a short list of decisions for Megan Spies. Page numbers are PDF pages (13 pages, letter, cover = p.1).
