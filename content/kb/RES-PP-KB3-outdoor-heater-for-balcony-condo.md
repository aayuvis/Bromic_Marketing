On most balconies and condo terraces the right outdoor heater is electric. It has no flame and no propane cylinder, and it mounts on the wall or the soffit above you, leaving the floor free. The fire code many US cities adopt keeps open-flame cooking devices off wooden balconies of apartment buildings, allowing only the smallest 2.5 lb propane bottles, and an HOA can be stricter, so start with your building's written policy.[^s1]

## Electric Or Propane Up High

| | Electric infrared (wall or soffit) | Propane (freestanding or tabletop) |
|---|---|---|
| Flame and fuel | None | Open flame, cylinder kept on the balcony |
| Fuel on a wooden balcony | None | For grills, the code allows only a 2.5 lb bottle and no larger cylinder kept there[^s1] |
| Building or HOA rules | Usually an electrical-works approval | Can be stricter than the code[^s1] |
| Floor space | None used | Takes a corner of the floor |

That rule is written for grills, and cities adopt their own editions, so ask your fire department how it treats a propane heater before you buy one. [[VERIFY: whether the IFC open-flame and LP-gas sections are applied to patio heaters on multifamily balconies — Specification and compliance]]

## A Heater That Fits A Small Space

A balcony table for four needs about 80 ft² of warmth at 20 ft² a seat, and one heater often covers it. Tungsten Smart-Heat® Electric 2000 W covers about 65 ft² and Platinum Smart-Heat® Electric 3400 W about 108 ft².[^s2][^s3] Wall mounts sit at least 72 in above the floor and ceiling mounts at least 96 in.[^s2][^s3] On a city terrace like the one pictured, heaters set into the soffit keep the view clear.

Pair the heater with Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls: an On/Off or Dimmer controller, or a manual wall switch. App schedules and voice control need compatible Affinity controls, sold separately.[^s4]

Some multifamily buildings plan this for residents: an all-electric building in Albany, New York, warms its shared roof deck with Eclipse freestanding heaters on a dedicated outlet built into the deck, because gas was never an option.[^s5]

## Questions For Your Building Before You Buy

- Is a new 208 V or 240 V circuit to the balcony allowed, and who approves the electrical work? Bromic electric heaters run on 208–240 V.[^s2][^s3]
- May anything be fixed to the facade or soffit, and in what finish?
- Is there an outdoor-rated junction point already, or a conduit route from the unit's panel? [[VERIFY: typical condo electrical approval steps — US field sales]]

All wiring is done by a licensed electrician and confirmed with your local building authority and your building's management.
