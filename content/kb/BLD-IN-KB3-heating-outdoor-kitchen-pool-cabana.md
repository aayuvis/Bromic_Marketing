Plan the heater in the same rough-in as the kitchen's appliances and lighting. In an outdoor kitchen or pool cabana the usual answer is electric infrared in the ceiling, aimed at the seating and the cook's station, on its own circuit with its controller located before the ceiling closes.[^s1][^s2] Ceiling mounts sit at least 96 in above the floor, and the space must keep at least 30% of its perimeter open to count as outdoor.[^s1]

## Where The Heat Goes

| Zone | Heater placement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bar seating and island | Ceiling-mounted or recessed over the guest side | Warmth on the guests, not the grill |
| Lounge or fireplace wall | Ceiling over the seating, or wall-mounted at least 72 in up[^s1] | Pairs with the fireplace on the coldest nights |
| Pool cabana | One heater over each cabana's seating | The Calile Hotel in Brisbane gives each of its seven poolside cabanas its own overhead Platinum heater, controlled from inside[^s4] |
| Pool deck, open | Pole-mounted or freestanding | Keep heaters back from splash zones [[VERIFY: guidance on distance from pool edge and IP rating for splash zones — Product team]] |

Tungsten Smart-Heat® Electric 4000 W and 6000 W carry two separately controllable elements, so one heater can run at half output while the kitchen is busy and full output once everyone sits down.[^s2]

## Rough-In Checklist

- **Circuits.** One dedicated 208 V or 240 V circuit per heater group, sized by a licensed electrician to the heater's amps (NFPA 70).[^s1][^s2]
- **Controller location.** A dry, reachable spot for the Affinity Smart-Heat® On/Off or Dimmer controller, within Bluetooth range of where people stand; each channel carries up to 6 kW at 208–277 V.[^s3]
- **Wall switch.** A manual wall switch box by the kitchen entry if the homeowner wants one.
- **Ceiling framing.** Blocking at each heater position, or space for a recess kit where the heater sits flush.[^s1] See [recessing into a soffit](item:ARC-IN-KB1).
- **Clearances.** Hold the manual's distances to combustible ceilings and to the hood. [[VERIFY: clearance between a heater and an outdoor kitchen vent hood — Product team]]
- **App.** Schedules, zones and voice need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately.[^s5]

Confirm the design and permit with the local building authority before the ceiling closes.
