A standard US outlet supplies 120 V, and a heater on an ordinary 15 A household circuit tops out at about 1,500 W, which is why nearly every plug-in patio heater sits at that figure.[^s1] Built-in infrared heaters run on a 208 V or 240 V circuit and deliver 2,000–6,000 W, enough to warm a whole table rather than one chair.[^s1][^s2] For a seating area you will use often, plan the circuit.

## What Each Voltage Gives You

| | 120 V plug-in | 208 V / 240 V built-in (Bromic electric) |
|---|---|---|
| Typical output | About 1,500 W[^s1] | Tungsten 2,000–6,000 W; Platinum 2,300–4,500 W[^s2][^s3] |
| Warmth for | One or two seats | 65–160 ft² of seating per heater[^s2][^s3] |
| Supply | Any outdoor outlet | A dedicated circuit from the panel, run by a licensed electrician |
| Mounting | Tabletop, stand or bracket | Wall, ceiling, pole or recessed |
| Controls | On-unit switch | Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller, or a manual wall switch |

A 240 V plug will not fit a 120 V outlet, and adapters are never the answer.[^s1]

## Does Running On 240 V Cost More?

The bill follows watts and hours, not voltage. A 3,400 W heater uses 3.4 kWh in an hour whether it is fed at 208 V or 240 V; it simply draws more amps at the lower voltage. The Platinum 4500 W, for example, draws 18.8 A at 240 V and 21.6 A at 208 V, which is why your electrician sizes the breaker to your supply.[^s3] See [breaker size for a 4500 W heater](item:BLD-IN-KB1) and [how many heaters a circuit can carry](item:BLD-IN-KB2).

## Where Each One Fits

- **A balcony chair or a quick warm-up:** a 120 V plug-in can be enough.
- **A dining table, lounge or covered patio:** a built-in heater on its own circuit. Tungsten Smart-Heat® Electric and Platinum Smart-Heat® Electric are hard-wired and paired with Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls: an On/Off or Dimmer controller, or a manual wall switch.[^s2][^s3]
- **A spot with no wall or ceiling to mount on:** Eclipse Smart-Heat® Freestanding runs at 2,900 W on 220–240 V and comes with its Eclipse Dimmer Controller and remote.[^s4] [[VERIFY: Eclipse Freestanding plug type and whether a 240 V outdoor receptacle is required — Product team]]

App schedules, zones and voice control need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately.[^s5] Every new circuit is a job for a licensed electrician; confirm the work and any permit with your local building authority.
