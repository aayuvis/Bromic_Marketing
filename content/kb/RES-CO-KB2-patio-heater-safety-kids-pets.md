They can be, when the heater is the right type, installed to code and kept out of reach. A built-in electric heater mounted on a wall at least 72" up, or a ceiling at least 96" up, sits above the play zone and burns no fuel.[^s1] Freestanding heaters need more care: keep children and pets out of a three-foot zone around them and supervise while they run.[^s3] [[VERIFY: NFPA tip wording read on nfpa.org — Megan Spies]]

## What makes one setup safer than another?

| Factor | Safer choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Position | Wall or ceiling mounted, or recessed | Bromic guidance: wall mounts ≥72", ceiling ≥96" above the floor[^s1] |
| Fuel near enclosed spaces | Electric | No combustion; gas or propane in an enclosed space risks carbon monoxide[^s4] |
| Certification | A listed mark: Intertek, UL or CSA | Required for gas and electric appliances, portable heaters included[^s1] |
| Freestanding stability | Anti-tilt design or weighted base | Tungsten Freestanding Gas has an anti-tilt design; Eclipse Freestanding a weighted base[^s2] |
| Installation | Licensed electrician or gas fitter, to ANSI/NFPA 70 or ANSI Z223.1/NFPA 54 | Codes cover placement, supply and clearance to combustibles[^s1] |

## What should families watch for?

- **Hot surfaces.** Heating elements run at very high temperatures — a short-wave element is rated around 3,992°F in Bromic's guide.[^s1] Never let anyone touch a heater face, running or just switched off. [[VERIFY: cool-down time and housing surface temperatures by model — Mike Clifford]]
- **Kid-free zone.** NFPA advises a three-foot kid-free zone around space heaters and open fires, and keeping things that can burn — cushions, toys, blankets — at least three feet away.[^s3]
- **Pets.** Keep beds and bowls out of the heat path so animals can move away from the warmth. [[VERIFY: any Bromic manual guidance on pets — Mike Clifford]]
- **Switching off.** Schedules and timers in the Bromic Smart-Heat app end heat when the evening does; they need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately. Every system has a control — an Affinity On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller, or a wall switch.[^s2]

Confirm installation requirements with your local building authority and a licensed electrician or gas fitter.
