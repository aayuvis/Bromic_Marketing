Any place people pause outdoors can be warmed: a hotel arrival court, a valet stand, a drive-thru queue, a rooftop bar, an office courtyard, a stadium suite terrace, a golf lounge or a hospital garden[^s1]. Radiant heat aimed at where people stand or sit turns a space they hurry through into one where they are happy to wait, gather or stay.

## Planning Warmth For A Space People Pass Through

- **Mount higher where people stand.** Our guidance is about 12 ft over walkways and queues, roughly 6 ft above head height; see [heating comfort zones](item:ARC-CO-KB3).
- **Warm the pause, rather than the whole path.** Concentrate heaters where people wait: the kerb, the stand, the door, the order window.
- **Mind the light where food is served.** Short wave's strong red glow can change how a dish looks on the plate[^s1]; Bromic electric heaters are medium wave, with a softer light. [[VERIFY: medium wave test data for no intrusive visual glow — Product team]] [[VERIFY: short wave visible glow and glare — Product team]]
- **Schedule it.** Pair each heater with Affinity Smart-Heat® controls and a wall switch, and set arrival zones to run on app schedules and timers around opening hours. Wi-Fi and app features require compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately[^s7].
