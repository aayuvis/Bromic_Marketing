Yes. Most patio heaters can be used on a wood or composite deck, provided the heater keeps the clearances its manual sets from the deck boards, rails, siding and anything overhead.[^s1] On most decks a heater mounted on the house wall or under the eaves is the simplest answer: nothing stands on the boards, and the warmth falls on the seating. Some local authorities restrict heaters on wooden decks, so check first.[^s1]

![Tungsten Electric heaters mounted under the eaves above a deck lounge](img:w-juneau-showhome-1)

## Which Heater Suits Your Deck

| Deck | Heater | Why it works | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Against the house, with a wall or eave | Tungsten or Platinum Smart-Heat® Electric, wall-mounted at least 72 in above the deck[^s2] | No flame, nothing on the boards | Affinity On/Off or Dimmer controller, or manual wall switch |
| Under a pergola or roof | The same heaters, ceiling-mounted at least 96 in up[^s2] | Warmth from above, floor kept clear | As above |
| Open deck, nothing to mount on | Eclipse Smart-Heat® Freestanding (electric, weighted base, wheels)[^s4] | Moves with the furniture | Eclipse Dimmer Controller and remote included[^s5] |
| Open deck with a gas supply | Tungsten Smart-Heat® Freestanding Gas (anti-tilt design)[^s4] | Open air only; rated to 8 mph wind[^s3] | [[VERIFY: freestanding gas controls — Product team]] |

## Before You Switch It On

- **Clearances.** Follow the installation manual for distances to the deck surface, rails and anything above. Eclipse Freestanding lists a 79 in minimum clearance.[^s5] [[VERIFY: Eclipse Freestanding clearance meaning (to ceiling or overall) and Tungsten Freestanding Gas clearances to combustibles — Product team]]
- **Footing.** A freestanding heater stands on a level, firm part of the deck, out of the walkway, never against a rail.
- **Supply.** Electric heaters need an outdoor-rated circuit run by a licensed electrician (NFPA 70); gas lines are a job for a licensed gas fitter (ANSI Z223.1/NFPA 54).[^s2]
- **Controls.** App schedules and zones need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately.[^s4]
- **Local rules.** Confirm with your local building authority or fire department before installing on a wooden deck.[^s1][^s2]
