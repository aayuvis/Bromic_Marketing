Yes. Bromic electric heaters can be recessed flush into a soffit or ceiling cavity using a Bromic recess kit, which fits Platinum and Tungsten Electric heaters[^s1]. The recess has to be designed: the opening, the cavity and the circuit belong on the drawings before the ceiling closes.

| Heater | Recessing | Coverage (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum Smart-Heat Electric (2300/3400/4500W) | Recess kit | 65–143 ft² |
| Tungsten Smart-Heat Electric, Select (coming soon) | Low-clearance recessing | 64–144 ft² |
| Tungsten Smart-Heat Electric, Coastal (coming soon) | Marine-grade low-clearance recessing | 64–144 ft² |

Source: Lookbook 2026 and Planning Guide[^s1][^s2].

## What to draw and confirm

- [ ] Cut-out size and position on the reflected ceiling plan
- [ ] Cavity depth, framing, trimmers and blocking
- [ ] Any clearance or ventilation required inside the recess, from the installation manual
- [ ] Conduit route and junction box location
- [ ] Mounting height in section (ceiling mounts at least 96 in, varying with output)[^s1]
- [ ] Controller and wall switch location

[[VERIFY: recess kit cut-out, cavity depth and in-cavity clearance per model — Mike Clifford]]

Specify the controls with each recessed heater: an Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off or Dimmer controller, a wall switch and the Bromic Smart-Heat app. Wi-Fi and app features require compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately[^s2].

Gas heaters are not recessed this way; they use heat deflectors and ceiling poles instead[^s1].

*Confirm installation details with the manual, the authority having jurisdiction and a licensed electrician.*
