Size a patio heater from the seating you want warm. Measure the seating area, then divide it by the heater's published coverage, adjusted for how covered and how cold your patio is, and round up.[^s3] A 12 × 16 ft seating area under a pergola on cool evenings needs two Tungsten Smart-Heat® Electric 4000 W heaters, or three Platinum Smart-Heat® Electric 3400 W.

## Why A Watts-Per-Square-Foot Rule Misleads

Radiant heat warms the people in its path, not the air, so the useful number is the area a heater covers at its mounting height. Bromic's own coverage figures work out to roughly 31–38 W for each square foot of seating, but the published coverage is the figure to use, because height, aim and cover change it.[^s1][^s2]

| Heater | Output | Coverage (approx.) | Works out to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tungsten Electric | 2000 W | 65 ft²[^s1] | 31 W/ft² |
| Tungsten Electric | 4000 W | 130 ft²[^s1] | 31 W/ft² |
| Tungsten Electric | 6000 W | 160 ft²[^s1] | 38 W/ft² |
| Platinum Electric | 3400 W | 108 ft²[^s2] | 31 W/ft² |
| Platinum Electric | 4500 W | 143 ft²[^s2] | 31 W/ft² |

## The One Sum

heaters = seating area ÷ (coverage × cover factor × climate factor), rounded up[^s3]

| Cover | Factor | Evenings you heat | Factor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open | 0.85 | Mild (above about 50°F) | 1.0 |
| Partly covered (pergola, part roof) | 1.0 | Cool (about 40–50°F) | 0.85 |
| Covered (solid roof) | 1.1 | Cold (often below about 40°F) | 0.7 |

No seat count? Allow 20 ft² a seat, so six dining chairs need about 120 ft².[^s3]

## Worked Example: 12 × 16 ft, Partly Covered, Cool

- Seating area: 12 × 16 = 192 ft². Factors: 1.0 × 0.85 = 0.85.
- Tungsten Electric 4000 W: 192 ÷ (130 × 0.85) = 1.7, so **2 heaters**.
- Platinum Electric 3400 W: 192 ÷ (108 × 0.85) = 2.1, so **3 heaters**.

Each layout is paired with Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls: an On/Off or Dimmer controller sized to the total load, or a manual wall switch.[^s1][^s4] App schedules and voice need compatible Affinity controls, sold separately.[^s4] For where each heater goes, see [patio heater placement](item:RES-PP-01).
