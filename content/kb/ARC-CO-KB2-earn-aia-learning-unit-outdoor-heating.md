Bromic's lunch and learn, The Evolution of Outdoor Heating, is registered with AIA/CES as course AIA4041002 and earns one learning unit with HSW credit[^s1][^s2]. In about an hour, our specification team walks your studio through how warmth works outdoors, how to choose a fuel, and how to place, clear and control heaters so the space performs from the first evening.

## What The Hour Covers

The course is built around five learning objectives[^s1]. Each one has a fuller reference in the Hub for the project on your desk.

| Learning objective | Read further |
|---|---|
| The value of heat in extending how, and how long, an outdoor space is used | [Outdoor living design trends](item:ARC-AW-01) |
| Convection, conduction and infrared, and the short, medium and long wave bands | [Radiant heat outdoors](item:ARC-CO-01) |
| Choosing between natural gas, propane and electric for the site and the codes | [All-electric outdoor heating codes](item:ARC-PP-01) |
| Placement, mounting height, clearances, mounting methods and recessing | [Heating comfort zones](item:ARC-CO-KB3) and [specify heating early](item:BLOG-30) |
| Zoning, smart controls, building automation and electrification | [Integrating heaters with home automation and BMS](item:BLOG-09) |

## How The Session Runs

- **Format:** a one-hour presentation with time for questions, usually over lunch at your office[^s2]. [[VERIFY: virtual delivery, group size and lead time to book — Specification and compliance]]
- **Credit:** one LU with HSW, reported to AIA for each attendee. [[VERIFY: how attendance is recorded and reported to AIA, and whether IDCEC credit is also offered — Specification and compliance]]
- **Product-neutral:** as AIA/CES requires, the hour teaches method rather than a brand, and questions about Bromic products are welcome once it ends[^s1].

## Before You Book

- Choose a date and say how many people will attend, including any landscape architects, interior designers or MEP engineers on the team.
- Bring a live project if you have one; our team can follow the session with a heat plan for it.
- Register through the form on our [AIA continuing education page](https://www.bromic.com/aia-ce/).
