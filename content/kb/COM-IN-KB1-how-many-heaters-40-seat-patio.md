It depends on the area the 40 seats occupy, not the seat count. Measure the seating zone in square feet, divide by each heater's coverage, round up, then add units for wind, open sides and a cold climate. On an illustrative 800 ft² patio that means about four to seven heaters, depending on the model.

## The method

1. **Measure** the area you want warm — the tables and chairs, not the planters.
2. **Divide** by the heater's coverage (Bromic's published ranges below).[^s1]
3. **Round up** to whole heaters.
4. **Adjust** for climate and exposure. Bromic's own example: the same 200 ft² patio takes two heaters in California and three in Massachusetts.[^s2] A partial cover helps; an open, windy edge needs more.

## Worked example

Illustrative only: assumes 40 seats occupy 800 ft². Measure yours.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Platinum Smart-Heat Electric | 65–143 ft² | 5.6–12.3 | 6–13 | Affinity On/Off or Dimmer controller, wall switch |
| Eclipse Smart-Heat Electric | about 121 ft² | 6.6 | 7 | Eclipse controller, wall switch |
| Tungsten Smart-Heat Freestanding Gas | about 215 ft² | 3.7 | 4 | [[VERIFY: control options — Mike Clifford]] |
