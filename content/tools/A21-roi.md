## More Nights Of Full Tables

When the patio stays warm, the evenings you once lost to the cold become nights your guests can stay outside. This calculator shows
an operator what heating the patio could add: the extra revenue from nights they could not seat outside before, the margin on it,
and how many months the installed cost takes to pay back.

You answer seven questions; we size the heaters from the seats and take the electricity price from your state (EIA). The result
card shows a seat grid, where each added dollar goes, the cash position month by month with the break-even point, and payback at
other shares of seats filled.

## What You Tell Us

Heated seats · average check · turns a night · contribution margin % · extra evenings a year · share of seats filled ·
state (electricity price). Under "Use your own numbers" you can set heaters (sized from the seats by templates/calculation-model.md),
installed cost a heater (placeholder), heated hours an evening and the electricity price.

## What You Discover

On screen you see the added revenue a year and payback in months. When you share your details, the full report adds the margin, a
sensitivity view and the heaters and controls the installed cost should cover (Affinity Smart-Heat controller, wall switch, app).
