A patio heater is worth it when you already love your outdoor space and lose it to cool evenings. A built-in electric heater costs about $0.42–$0.82 an hour to run at US average rates, warms the seating within seconds and gives back the spring and fall evenings that usually end indoors.[^s4][^s5] If the patio is rarely used even in summer, a heater will not change that.

## The Evenings A Patio Usually Loses

Most new homes now come with a patio: 60.5% of single-family homes started in 2025 had one.[^s1] Far fewer come with the warmth to enjoy it once the sun goes down. In a survey Bromic commissioned, 67% of people said heating would do the most to extend their time outside, yet only 23% rank outdoor heating a top priority when they plan.[^s2][^s3] That gap is where most patios fall quiet after dark.

Radiant warmth closes it. An infrared heater warms the people and the furniture in its path, not the air, so it keeps working in a light breeze, and Bromic's medium wave electric heaters are warm in about 30 seconds.[^s5] Their warmth reaches further across the table than short or long wave, with no intrusive glow. ## When A Heater Earns Its Place

| Your outdoor space | Worth it? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Used most summer evenings, empty from October | Yes | The heater adds the shoulder seasons to evenings you already enjoy |
| Covered patio, porch or pergola | Yes, and it works hardest here | A cover holds a "heat cloud" over the seating[^s5] |
| Open deck in a windy spot | Yes, with the right heater and placement | Radiant heat copes with light wind; place it out of the gusts[^s5] |
| Rarely used, even in summer | Probably not yet | Warmth extends a habit; it seldom creates one |
| Fully enclosed sunroom | Not an outdoor heater | A space needs at least 30% of its perimeter open to count as outdoor[^s5] |

## What It Costs To Own And Run

Running cost is the smallest line. At 18.3¢ a kWh, a Platinum Smart-Heat® Electric 3400 W heater costs about $0.62 an hour at full output; three hours a night for 100 nights is about $187 a season.[^s4] Our guide to [what a patio heater costs to run](item:RES-IN-01) has the formulas for electric, natural gas and propane.

The larger decision is the setup, and the right one depends on how you live outside.

Built-in heaters are the quietest in the design: nothing on the floor, warmth from above, and on the Tungsten collection a seven-year warranty on the elements.[^s7]

## Questions That Tell You

- **How many evenings would you add?** Count the months you would sit outside if it were warm enough.
- **Where do you sit?** Measure the seating, not the slab; that sets the number of heaters. See [what size patio heater you need](item:RES-CO-KB4).
- **Is there a wall, ceiling or beam to mount on?** If so, built-in electric is usually the most elegant answer.
- **Are you building or renovating?** Wiring while the ceiling is open costs far less than retrofitting later.[^s5]
- **How will you control it?** Every heater is paired with its controls; app schedules and voice need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately.[^s6]

## What To Take Away

- A patio heater pays its way on a patio you already use, by adding the cool evenings around it.
- Electric infrared costs well under a dollar an hour to run at average US rates.
- Built-in heaters suit covered spaces; freestanding heaters suit open ones.
- Plan the heater, its circuit and its controls together, ideally before the ceiling is finished.

## FAQ

### Do patio heaters really work?
Yes, within their coverage. Infrared heaters warm the people and furniture in their path rather than the air, so you feel the warmth even on a cool, breezy evening.[^s5]

### Are patio heaters expensive to run?
Not usually. A 3400 W electric heater costs about $0.62 an hour at the July 2026 US average electricity price.[^s4]

### Is a patio heater worth it for a small patio?
Often more so. One built-in heater of 65–160 ft² can warm a small dining or lounge area.[^s7]

### Is a fire pit or a patio heater better?
They do different jobs. A fire pit is a gathering point; an overhead heater warms every seat evenly, and many outdoor spaces use both.

### Does a patio heater add value to a home?
We have not found a sourced figure for resale value, so we do not claim one. What it adds for certain is more evenings outside.

### Do I need an electrician?
For a built-in electric heater, yes: a licensed electrician runs its circuit, and your local building authority confirms any permit.
