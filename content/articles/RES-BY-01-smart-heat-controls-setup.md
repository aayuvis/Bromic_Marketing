Bromic heaters work with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant through compatible Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, which connect them to the Bromic Smart-Heat app.[^s1] Once everything is set up, you can bring the warmth on with a word, group heaters into zones and schedule them around your evenings. Wi-Fi, app and voice features need compatible Affinity controls, sold separately, and without them your heaters run from a manual wall switch.[^s1]

## Everything You Need For App And Voice Control

Your system builds up in layers, from the wall upwards.

| Layer | What it is | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| Heater | Platinum, Tungsten Electric (Coastal and Select, coming soon) or Eclipse | The heat |
| Controller | Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller; or a manual wall switch[^s1] | On/off; Dimmer adds output levels; Eclipse controller runs Eclipse heat and light |
| Bromic Smart-Heat app | Phone app, with compatible Affinity controls[^s1] | Adjust heat, set timers and schedules, manage zones from anywhere[^s1] |
| Voice and smart home | Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, Control4, Crestron, Lutron[^s1] | Hands-free and whole-home control; compatibility varies and may need extra integration hardware or configuration[^s1] |

A manual wall switch is a complete, working control on its own, though it does not connect to the app or to voice assistants. [[VERIFY: which Affinity controllers are Wi-Fi enabled, and which heaters each controller supports — Product team]]

## Creating Zones Around The Way You Live Outdoors

A zone is a group of heaters you control together, such as the dining table, the lounge or the outdoor kitchen. The app lets you control several areas independently.[^s1]

Shape your zones around how you use the space, and let the wiring follow:

- **A zone for each part of the evening.** Dinner at the table, then a drink by the fire later, with the warmth following you from one to the next.
- **Divide long spaces.** A long terrace with heaters along one side can become two zones, so a couple relaxing at one end leaves the rest of the run off.
- **Give wind-exposed edges their own zone.** You may want them turned higher when the breeze picks up.

Zones depend on the wiring and controllers installed, so settle them before the electrician begins. [[VERIFY: heaters per controller and zones per app account — Product team]] See [where to place patio heaters](item:RES-PP-01).

## Schedules And Timers That Follow Your Evenings

Schedules switch heaters on and off at set times, and timers switch them off after a set period.[^s1] Together they keep the warmth in step with your routine:

- **Schedule your usual evenings.** Warm the dining zone from 6:30 to 9:30 on weeknights, and from late afternoon at weekends.
- **Add a timer for the extras.** A 60-minute timer for a nightcap switches the heat off once everyone has gone inside.
- **Adjust with the seasons.** Move your schedules as sunset shifts through the year.

Bromic designs schedules as a way to match heat to your routine and save energy.[^s1] Fewer heated hours is the single biggest lever on [running cost](item:RES-IN-01).

## Dimming The Warmth To Suit The Night

Heaters run by a Dimmer or Eclipse controller can be dimmed. Eclipse heaters step between 33%, 66% and 100% output, and their rim lighting dims as well.[^s1] An On/Off controller or wall switch gives full heat or none. [[VERIFY: Dimmer controller output levels for Platinum and Tungsten Electric — Product team]]

Dimming matters outdoors because there is no room temperature for a thermostat to hold.[^s2] You set the level to the evening itself, gentle on a mild night and full when the wind picks up.

The Lookbook confirms that Alexa and Google Assistant work with Bromic heaters through compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls.[^s1] The general order is:

1. Install compatible Affinity controls and connect them to Wi-Fi. [[VERIFY: connection method and network requirements — Product team]]

## Bringing Heat Into Control4, Crestron Or Lutron

Affinity controls are listed as compatible with Control4, Crestron and Lutron. Compatibility varies by system and may need additional integration hardware or configuration, so bring your integrator in early.[^s1]

## Your First Week With Smart-Heat

The first week is when your controls become part of everyday life. Work through this list once, while your installer is still on call.

- **On day one,** test every heater from its controller or wall switch, and note which switch runs which heater.
- **Name the zones** in the app after the places you gather – table, lounge, kitchen – rather than the circuit numbers.
- **Set one weekday schedule and one weekend schedule,** then fine-tune them after a few evenings outside.
- **Add a timer** for late nights, so the heat stops when you go in.
- **Link your voice assistant** and try a few commands from the patio itself, where you will use them most.
- **Show everyone at home** how to switch the heat on and off by hand, without the app.
- **Keep the installation manual** and the controller model numbers with your house documents.

## What To Take Away

- Every heater is paired with a control: an Affinity On/Off, Dimmer or Eclipse controller, or a manual wall switch.
- App, schedules, zones and voice need compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately.[^s1]

- Schedules and timers trim wasted hours, and dimming matches the heat to the night.
- Alexa and Google Assistant work through the app, while Control4, Crestron and Lutron may need extra hardware.[^s1]
