The most reliable way to heat a rooftop bar or hotel terrace is electric infrared: short-wave heaters on the walls, ceilings or poles over every seat, with freestanding heaters where there is nothing to mount on. Radiant heat warms guests rather than air, so it holds up in the breeze a roof always has, and it needs no flame or fuel stored on the deck.[^s5] Bromic's rooftop projects follow the same plan.[^s1][^s2]

## Why Roofs Are Different

A terrace at street level is sheltered by its neighbors. A roof has wind, a view nobody wants blocked, and often a building that will not take gas.

| Rooftop challenge | What it means for heating | How Bromic projects answered it |
|---|---|---|
| Wind | Convective heat is blown away; long-wave heat is the most wind-sensitive[^s5] | Electric infrared chosen for the wind at Hive and Honey, 16 stories up, and at Hyatt Regency Dehradun's rooftop bar[^s1][^s2] |
| Skyline views | Heaters on tall ceilings can sit in the sightline | Freestanding heaters over the lounges, white Tungsten Electric that blends with the ceiling[^s1] |
| Window-washing rigs | Nothing on the perimeter; furniture must clear | Eclipse freestanding heaters that move aside on cleaning days[^s1] |
| All-electric building | Gas fire tables and heaters ruled out | Eclipse freestanding heaters on a dedicated outlet built into the deck[^s3] |
| No ceiling or wall | No mounting point | Eclipse Smart-Heat® Freestanding, heat and light in one[^s2] |

Freestanding gas suits sheltered open-air spaces; the Tungsten Smart-Heat® Freestanding Gas is rated to 8 mph wind, which many roofs exceed.[^s8]

## Planning A 40-Seat Rooftop Lounge

Use the seating, at 20 ft² a seat, and the factors for an open roof on cool evenings (0.85 × 0.85 = 0.72).

- **Area:** 40 seats × 20 ft² = 800 ft².
- **Tungsten Smart-Heat® Electric 6000 W on poles or a canopy:** 800 ÷ (160 × 0.72) = 6.9, so **7 heaters**.[^s6]
- **Eclipse Smart-Heat® Freestanding where nothing can be mounted:** 800 ÷ (121 × 0.72) = 9.2, and 40 seats ÷ 4 = 10, so **10 heaters**.[^s7]

| Layout | Heaters | Controls |
|---|---|---|
| 7 × Tungsten Electric 6000 W | 7 | One heater a channel at 6 kW: a 5-channel and a 2-channel Affinity Dimmer or On/Off controller[^s7] |
| 10 × Eclipse Freestanding | 10 | Eclipse Dimmer Controller and remote with each heater[^s7] |
| Mixed: Tungsten over the bar, Eclipse in the lounge | By zone | As above, one zone per controller channel |

Tungsten 6000 W carries two separately controllable elements and runs on 277 V as well as 208–240 V, which suits a commercial building's supply.[^s6] For zones and schedules from a phone, choose compatible Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, sold separately; the expansion board links them to a building management system.[^s7][^s9]

## Before The Terrace Opens

- Bring heating into the rooftop drawings early: circuits, mounting points and controller locations.
- Plan zones by how the space is sold: bar, lounge, private hire.
- Electric work follows NFPA 70 and any gas work ANSI Z223.1/NFPA 54.[^s5] Rooftop gas often faces extra limits from the fire marshal. Confirm every installation with the local building authority and fire department and a licensed electrician or gas fitter. ## Key Takeaways

- Electric infrared is the dependable choice for a roof: it copes with wind and needs no fuel on the deck.[^s5]
- Mount heaters where they keep the view; use freestanding heaters where nothing can be fixed.
- A 40-seat open rooftop on cool evenings needs about 7 Tungsten 6000 W or 10 Eclipse Freestanding heaters.
- Every layout is paired with its controls, zoned the way the terrace is used.

## FAQ

### Do patio heaters work on a windy rooftop?
Radiant electric heaters do: they warm guests, not air, so a breeze carries less of the warmth away. Shelter the heater from the strongest gusts and size it for an open space.[^s5]

### Can you use propane heaters on a rooftop bar?
It depends on your fire marshal and building, and many all-electric buildings rule gas out entirely.[^s3] Ask the local fire department before planning gas on a roof.

### How many heaters does a rooftop bar need?
Measure the seating at 20 ft² a seat and divide by each heater's coverage, adjusted for an open roof and the season. A 40-seat open lounge needs about 7 Tungsten Electric 6000 W heaters.[^s6]

### What heaters work where nothing can be mounted?
Freestanding electric heaters such as Eclipse Smart-Heat® Freestanding, which come with an Eclipse Dimmer Controller and remote.[^s7]

### Can the heaters connect to the hotel's building management system?
Yes. Affinity Smart-Heat controllers link to a BMS through the expansion board.[^s7]
