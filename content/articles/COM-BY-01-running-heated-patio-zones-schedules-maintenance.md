Maintain commercial patio heaters with a short routine and the manufacturer's manual: keep the face and grille clean, check mounts, cords and gas connections on a schedule, and have a licensed electrician or gas fitter service anything beyond cleaning. The bigger win is operating them well — zones that match your sections, schedules that follow the book, and one SOP every shift uses, so heaters are never left on and every table gets the same warmth.

## Why do heated patios go wrong?

Three reasons, all operational: heaters left on after close, staff unsure which switch runs which area, and guests at one table roasting while the next is cold. Outdoor heat cannot be thermostatically controlled,[^s2] so the control system and the people using it do the work a thermostat does indoors.

## How should a patio be zoned?

Zone by how you serve, not by where the wiring runs.

| Zone | Typical area | Why separate |
|---|---|---|
| Bar rail and standing | Along the bar or counter | Busy early, empties late |
| Main dining | Core table section | Follows reservations |
| Edge and overflow | Perimeter and windward side | Opens only when busy or windy |
| Lounge or waiting | Soft seating, host stand | Short dwell, lower output |
| Event or private | Buyout area | Runs on its own schedule |

Each zone needs its own controller. For Platinum and Tungsten Electric that means an Affinity Smart-Heat On/Off or Dimmer controller plus a wall switch; for Eclipse, the Eclipse controller (Tungsten Coastal and Select are coming soon). The Bromic Smart-Heat app adds zones, schedules and timers. Wi-Fi and app features require compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls, sold separately.[^s1] Sites with a building-management system can integrate with Control4, Crestron or Lutron; compatibility varies and may need extra hardware.[^s1]

## How should heaters be scheduled?

Tie heat to covers.

- **Pre-open:** short-wave and medium-wave heaters warm in seconds,[^s2] so switch zones on as the first tables are seated, not an hour before.
- **Service:** run main dining on the reservation curve; bring edge zones on only when seated.
- **Step output:** use dimming or Eclipse's 33/66/100% steps on milder nights.[^s1]
- **Last table:** timers switch each zone off a set time after the zone's last cover.
- **Close:** a hard schedule turns everything off at closing, whatever the staff remember.

## What should the shift SOP say?

One page, at the host stand and in the app.

**Opening**
1. Visual check: heaters undamaged, grilles clear, nothing hung on or near them.
2. Confirm clearances: umbrellas, banners and décor away from heaters per the manual.
3. Portable gas: heater upright, wheels locked, at least 5 ft from buildings, awnings and exits where the IFC applies.[^s3]
4. Switch on by zone as tables are seated.

**During service**
5. Adjust output by zone, not by moving guests.
6. Report any odour, flicker, tripped breaker or damage to the manager; switch off that unit.

**Closing**
7. Confirm all zones off in the app or at the wall switch.
8. Portable gas: close the cylinder valve and store per your site plan.
9. Log faults for the maintenance contractor.

## What is the cleaning and maintenance checklist?

Generic guidance; your model's installation and operating manual takes precedence. [[VERIFY: Bromic recommended maintenance intervals and cleaning method per model — Mike Clifford]]

| Task | Who | Frequency (per manual) |
|---|---|---|
| Wipe housing and fascia when cool | Staff | Weekly in season |
| Check grille and element area for debris, cobwebs, nests | Staff | Weekly in season |
| Inspect brackets, poles and mounting fixings | Manager | Monthly |
| Check cords, wall switches and controllers for damage | Manager | Monthly |
| Gas: check hoses, regulator and connections | Licensed gas fitter | Per manual, and at season start |
| Electrical: check circuits and connections | Licensed electrician | Per manual, and at season start |
| Coastal sites: rinse salt from stainless surfaces | Staff | Per manual |
| Off-season: cover or store portable units | Staff | End of season |

Never clean a hot heater, never spray water into an element, and never open a sealed unit. Anything beyond surface cleaning goes to a licensed trade.

## How do you standardise across sites?

Make every site the same, so a manager who moves sites already knows the system.

| Standardise | Why |
|---|---|
| Product family and finish per site type | One spare-parts list, one look |
| Zone naming (Bar, Dining, Edge, Lounge, Event) | Same words in every app and SOP |
| Controller types | One training module |
| Schedule templates by season | Head office sets, sites adjust |
| SOP and maintenance log | Comparable records across the estate |
| Permit pack | Repeatable filings for new sites — see [the permit checklist](item:COM-PP-01) |

Start the next site from the last one's layout and adjust for exposure. Bromic's design service will draw coverage for each new site.[^s1]

## Key takeaways

- Zone by service area; one controller per zone.
- Schedules and timers do what a thermostat cannot outdoors.
- One-page SOP for opening, service and close.
- Clean weekly in season; trades service gas and electrics per the manual.
- Standardise products, zone names and SOPs across every site.

## FAQ

### How do I maintain commercial patio heaters?

Clean housings and grilles when cool, inspect mounts, cords and connections on a schedule, and have a licensed electrician or gas fitter service them per the manufacturer's manual.

### How often should patio heaters be serviced?

Follow your model's manual. As a minimum, a trade inspection at the start of each season and a staff check weekly while in use.

### Can I schedule patio heaters automatically?

Yes, with compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls and the Bromic Smart-Heat app, which offers zones, schedules and timers. Wi-Fi and app features require those controls, sold separately.

### How many zones does a restaurant patio need?

Usually one per service area — bar, dining, edge, lounge — so heat follows covers. Small patios may need only two.

### Can heaters integrate with our building system?

Affinity controls work with Control4, Crestron and Lutron, among others; compatibility varies and may need extra integration hardware.
