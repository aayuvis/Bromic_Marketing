Caring for commercial patio heaters takes a short routine and the manufacturer's manual: keep the face and grille clean, check mounts, cords and gas connections on a schedule, and call a licensed electrician or gas fitter for anything beyond cleaning. The greater reward comes from running them well, with zones that match your sections, schedules that follow the book and one routine every shift shares, so each table enjoys the same warmth and no heater is left glowing after close.

## Where A Heated Patio Can Lose Its Way

The trouble is almost always operational. Heaters stay on after close, staff are unsure which switch runs which area, and one table feels too warm while the next is cold. Outdoor heat cannot be thermostatically controlled,[^s2] so your control system, and the team using it, take on the work a thermostat does indoors.

## Zoning Around The Way You Serve

Draw your zones around how the floor is served, and let the wiring follow.

| Zone | Typical area | Why separate |
|---|---|---|
| Bar rail and standing | Along the bar or counter | Busy early, empties late |
| Main dining | Core table section | Follows reservations |
| Edge and overflow | Perimeter and windward side | Opens only when busy or windy |
| Lounge or waiting | Soft seating, host stand | Short dwell, lower output |
| Event or private | Buyout area | Runs on its own schedule |

Each zone has its own controller. For Platinum and Tungsten Electric, Coastal and Select included, that means an Affinity Smart-Heat® On/Off or Dimmer controller with a wall switch, and for Eclipse, the Eclipse controller. The Bromic Smart-Heat app adds zones, schedules and timers. Wi-Fi and app features require compatible Affinity Smart-Heat® Controls, sold separately.[^s1] Sites with a building-management system can integrate with Control4, Crestron or Lutron; compatibility varies and may need extra hardware.[^s1]

## Scheduling Warmth To Follow Your Covers

Let the heat rise and fall with the evening's bookings.

- **Before opening:** Bromic's medium wave electric heaters warm in about 30 seconds,[^s2] so bring zones on as the first tables are seated rather than an hour ahead.
- **Through service:** run main dining on the reservation curve, and bring edge zones on once guests are seated there.
- **On milder nights:** use dimming or Eclipse's 33/66/100% steps to soften the output.[^s1]
- **After the last table:** timers switch each zone off a set time after its last cover.
- **At close:** a firm schedule turns everything off at closing, so nothing depends on memory at the end of a long night.

## A Shift Routine Everyone Can Follow

Keep it to one page, at the host stand and in the app.

**Opening**
1. Visual check: heaters undamaged, grilles clear, nothing hung on or near them.
2. Confirm clearances: umbrellas, banners and décor away from heaters per the manual.
3. Portable gas: heater upright, wheels locked, at least 5 ft from buildings, awnings and exits where the IFC applies.[^s3]
4. Switch on by zone as tables are seated.

**During service**
5. Adjust output by zone, so guests can stay where they are seated.
6. Report any odour, flicker, tripped breaker or damage to the manager, and switch off that unit.

**Closing**
7. Confirm all zones off in the app or at the wall switch.
8. Portable gas: close the cylinder valve and store per your site plan.
9. Log faults for the maintenance contractor.

## Caring For Your Heaters Season After Season

This is general guidance, and your model's installation and operating manual always takes precedence. [[VERIFY: Bromic recommended maintenance intervals and cleaning method per model — Product team]]

| Task | Who | Frequency (per manual) |
|---|---|---|
| Wipe housing and fascia when cool | Staff | Weekly in season |
| Check grille and element area for debris, cobwebs, nests | Staff | Weekly in season |
| Inspect brackets, poles and mounting fixings | Manager | Monthly |
| Check cords, wall switches and controllers for damage | Manager | Monthly |
| Gas: check hoses, regulator and connections | Licensed gas fitter | Per manual, and at season start |
| Electrical: check circuits and connections | Licensed electrician | Per manual, and at season start |
| Coastal sites: rinse salt from stainless surfaces | Staff | Per manual |
| Off-season: cover or store portable units | Staff | End of season |

Always let a heater cool before cleaning it, keep water away from the element, and leave sealed units closed. Anything beyond surface cleaning belongs with a licensed trade.

## Bringing The Same Experience To Every Site

When every site is set up the same way, a manager who moves between them already knows the system on day one.

| Standardise | Why |
|---|---|
| Product family and finish per site type | One spare-parts list, one look |
| Zone naming (Bar, Dining, Edge, Lounge, Event) | Same words in every app and SOP |
| Controller types | One training module |
| Schedule templates by season | Head office sets, sites adjust |
| SOP and maintenance log | Comparable records across the estate |
| Permit pack | Repeatable filings for new sites – see [the permit checklist](item:COM-PP-01) |

Begin each new site from the last one's layout and adjust for its exposure. Bromic's design service will draw the coverage for every new location.[^s1]

## What To Take Away

- Zone by service area, with one controller per zone.
- Schedules and timers do the work outdoors that a thermostat does inside.
- A one-page routine carries every shift through opening, service and close.
- Clean weekly in season, and have licensed trades service gas and electrics per the manual.
- Keep products, zone names and routines consistent across every site.

## FAQ

### How do I maintain commercial patio heaters?

Clean housings and grilles once they are cool, inspect mounts, cords and connections on a schedule, and have a licensed electrician or gas fitter service them per the manufacturer's manual.

### How often should patio heaters be serviced?

Follow your model's manual. As a minimum, plan a trade inspection at the start of each season and a staff check weekly while the heaters are in use.

### Can I schedule patio heaters automatically?

Yes, with compatible Affinity Smart-Heat Controls and the Bromic Smart-Heat app, which offers zones, schedules and timers. Wi-Fi and app features require those controls, sold separately.

### How many zones does a restaurant patio need?

Usually one per service area – bar, dining, edge, lounge – so the warmth follows your covers. A small patio may need only two.

### Can heaters integrate with our building system?

Affinity controls work with Control4, Crestron and Lutron, among others; compatibility varies and may need extra integration hardware.
