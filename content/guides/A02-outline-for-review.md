# Planning Guide v2: structure and changes

Three editions share one backbone — define the space, radiant heat, fuel, size, place, control, cost, act — and differ in depth and close. Controls are now a required step in all three. Every figure comes from the Lookbook 2026[^s2], the 2021 guide where still valid[^s1], or a cited EIA/NFPA/ICC source; unknowns are `[[VERIFY]]`.

## The three editions

| | Homeowner ([A02-homeowner](item:A02-homeowner)) | Professional ([A02-pro](item:A02-pro)) | Operator ([A02-commercial](item:A02-commercial)) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience / stage | RES · CO, IN | ARC, BLD · CO, IN | COM · CO, IN |
| Spine | 8 steps + one-page checklist | 11 technical sections + submittal checklist | 7 steps + multi-site checklist |
| Depth | Plain-language; 20 × 12 ft worked example | Wavelength table, codes, coverage/mounting matrix, amps at 208/240/277 V, accessories, integration | Revenue formula, 5-year TCO logic, zones/schedules, fire-code pointers, rollout |
| Cost content | Running cost per hour (EIA residential), all-in quote lines | Running cost per hour | 5-year energy per heater (EIA commercial), handling |
| CTA | Book a design consult → A07 | Download the spec kit → A11 | Get your ROI assessment → A21 |
| Length | ≈ 2,300 words | ≈ 2,300 words | ≈ 1,750 words |

Companion: **A16 Pre-wire guide** (BLD · IN) carries the rough-in detail the pro edition points to.

## What changed from v1.2 (2021)

| Topic | 2021 | v2 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Naming | Legacy numbered-series name | Tungsten, Platinum, Eclipse | Done |
| Range | No Eclipse, no app, Tungsten Portable | Platinum, Eclipse, Eclipse Freestanding, Tungsten Freestanding Gas, Tungsten Coastal/Select (coming soon), Affinity Controls and app | Done; Platinum Gas, Tungsten Gas built-in, Cobalt facts `[[VERIFY]]` |
| Tungsten Electric coverage | 65–108 ft² | 64–144 ft² | Flag to Mike Clifford |
| Platinum Electric coverage | 65–108 ft² | 65–143 ft² | Done |
| Tungsten gas output | 39,800 BTU | 38,000 BTU freestanding only; built-in not published | `[[VERIFY]]` |
| Running cost (200 ft²) | NG $0.40, propane $2.50, electric $0.88/h | Per-heater cost from EIA rates with formula shown | `[[VERIFY: 2026 rates — Brian Kim]]` |
| Controls | "Optional Step 08" | Required step in every edition; every recommendation names its control; Wi-Fi/app "sold separately" note | Done |
| Fire pits | Called out as insufficient | Kept, softened to one line | Done |
| Sizing example | 20 × 10 ft, CA vs MA, legacy series name | 20 × 12 ft covered patio, current models | Per-wattage coverage `[[VERIFY]]` |
| Electrical | 208/240/277 V listed | Amps per wattage, NEC 125% continuous-load rule, worked examples | Art. 424 vs 422 `[[VERIFY]]` |
| Codes | NFPA 70/54, 72"/96", certification | Same, plus primary links, IFC 603.4 and NFPA 58 pointers (commercial), disclaimer on every edition | IFC 2024 text `[[VERIFY]]` |
| Commercial | One paragraph | Full operator edition: revenue, TCO, zones, permits, rollout | Benchmarks `[[VERIFY: Blake Petty]]` |
| Design support | Phone, bromic.com/us | Design-your-space service, CTA per edition | Done |
